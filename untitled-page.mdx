---
title: "What is a Maintenance Contract"
description: "What a maintenance contract is, what it does automatically, and what you still need to do."
---

A maintenance contract is your servicing agreement with a building, written down in a form LiftAuth can act on. Once it is set up, LiftAuth creates the visits and drafts the invoices for you, every cycle.

<Note>
  Older guidance called this a "maintenance plan" and set it up per lift. It is now one contract per **building**, covering the lifts in it.
</Note>

## What a contract decides

You set these once, and LiftAuth uses them every cycle:

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Which lifts are covered** | The lifts at that building the agreement includes. |
| **Which forms are used** | The record templates the technician fills in on each visit. |
| **How often you visit** | The servicing frequency — monthly, quarterly, and so on. |
| **How it is billed** | The billing cadence and the amounts to invoice. |
| **The contract term** | When the agreement starts and ends. |

## What happens each cycle

Once a contract is active, this happens with no input from your team:

1. **A job is created 14 days before the visit date**, already assigned and time-slotted.
2. **It appears in the technician's app** so they can pick it up on the day.
3. **A draft invoice is generated** according to the billing cadence — per job when you close each job, or per period for a recurring fee.
4. **The draft waits in Billing** for someone to check and issue it.

## What you still do

LiftAuth handles the repetition. You handle the judgement.

- **Set the contract up**, once per building.
- **Review the record** before closing each job — check the technician's answers and photos look right.
- **Check and issue the draft invoice** before it reaches the building manager.

## A few important things

**Nothing is billed until you issue it.** A draft invoice is not a real document. It sits in Billing until somebody issues it, and issuing is separate again from emailing it to the customer.

**Backdating does not create history.** If you start a contract in the past, scheduling runs from the current month forward. Months already gone never generate jobs. To record visits that happened before you were on LiftAuth, use **Record past visits** instead.

**The building manager does not see the contract.** It is internal to your team. They see the visits, the records, and the invoices you issue.
