---
title: "How to reschedule and backfill visits"
description: "Move a single upcoming visit to a different date, or log visits that happened before the contract existed."
icon: "calendar-day"
---

A contract writes out every visit for its whole term in advance. Those visits live on the contract's **Upcoming Visits** tab until LiftAuth turns each one into a [job](/admin/jobs/overview), fourteen days before it is due.

Two things you will want to do with them: move one that lands badly, and fill in history that predates the contract.

## Move an upcoming visit

Use this when a single visit clashes with something — a public holiday, a site closure, a technician's leave. The rest of the schedule is untouched.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract">
    Go to **Maintenance Contracts** and click the row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the Upcoming Visits tab">
    Every future visit is listed with its date and how many lifts it covers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Reschedule on the visit you want to move">
    A dialog opens with the visit's current date and start time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the new date and, if you need to, a new start time">
    Times are Singapore time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Apply">
    The visit moves. Nothing else on the contract changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Reschedule only works on a visit that has not become a job yet. Once LiftAuth has created the job — roughly two weeks out — the **Reschedule** button is gone, and you move the **job** instead, from the job itself. You also cannot move a visit into the past, or on or after the contract's expiry date.
</Warning>

Rescheduling is available on **Active** contracts only. An expired or archived contract has no future visits left to move.

<Tip>
  If a visit is long enough to spill over into the next workday, each day of it can be moved on its own — the dialog is titled **Reschedule day 2**, and so on.
</Tip>

## Log visits that happened before the contract

Use this when you are moving an existing customer onto LiftAuth and want their service history in the system, or when the contract was backdated and those earlier months never generated jobs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract and stay on the Overview tab">
    The service-activity panel shows a year grid: solid marks are visits already recorded, dashed marks are visits planned under this contract.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Record past visits">
    You will only see this button if your role allows it — see [permissions](/admin/maintenance-contracts/reference#who-can-do-what).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a draft for each visit">
    Tick a month in the grid on the left to start a draft, or upload the signed PDFs and let LiftAuth read the service date, lifts and technician out of each one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check every draft">
    Set the date, the lifts covered and the technician. If the technician is not on LiftAuth, choose **Not on platform — enter a name** and type it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the visits">
    They are added as completed [records](/admin/records) against this contract, and the service-activity grid fills in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A month that already has a visit recorded is locked in the grid, so you cannot double-log the same month by accident.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Details read out of a PDF are a draft, not a fact. Read each one before you create it — the panel says so too.
</Warning>

## Where the rest of the history lives

- **Visit History** tab — every job this contract has produced, with its status.
- **Invoices** tab — every invoice raised off this contract. See [Billing](/admin/billing/overview) for what to do with them.
