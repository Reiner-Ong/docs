Everything the Maintenance Contracts area holds, for looking up rather than reading through. For how to actually do things, start at the [overview](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview).

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Can it be edited? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active** | The contract is inside its term and producing visits, jobs and invoices. | Yes |
| **Expired** | The contract has passed its expiry date. It produces nothing further. Existing jobs and invoices are unaffected. | No |
| **Archived** | Someone ended the contract early. All future visits were deleted. | No |

A contract becomes **Expired** on its own, on the nightly run, once the expiry date has passed. **Archived** is always a deliberate action.

<Note>
  **Expiring soon** is a filter, not a status. It picks out **Active** contracts that are more than 85% of the way through their term. Those show an amber expiry date in the list.
</Note>

## The contracts list

### Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Contract** | The contract reference, in the form `BP-2026-4F91C2` — `BP-`, the year, six characters. The contract page labels it **Contract No.**, and **Search contracts…** matches it. The `BP` is inherited from "Building Plan", the term this feature used before it was renamed to Maintenance Contracts. |
| **Building** | The building the contract covers. One contract, one building. |
| **Frequency** | **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Mixed** when its coverage groups do not all share a frequency. |
| **Lifts** | How many lifts the contract covers in total, across all its coverage groups. |
| **Assignee** | The technician or the team that works this contract's visits. |
| **Billing** | **Per job**, **Annual**, or **No invoicing**. |
| **Term** | A progress bar for the term, and the expiry month. Click the header to sort by expiry. |
| **Status** | Active, Expired or Archived. |

### Filters and search

- **Status buttons** — All, Active, Expiring soon, Expired, Archived. Each carries a count.
- **All frequencies** — narrow to Monthly, Quarterly or Mixed.
- **All assignees** — narrow to one technician or team.
- **Search contracts…** — matches the contract reference and the building name.

## The contract page

### Tabs

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The coverage groups, the schedule, the billing setup, certificate renewals, a year-by-year service-activity grid, and a short list of recent and upcoming visits. |
| **Upcoming Visits** | Every future visit the contract will produce, with **Reschedule** on the ones that are not jobs yet. |
| **Visit History** | Every [record](/admin/records) held for this contract's **building** — job-produced and hand-backfilled alike. It is not a list of jobs, and it is not scoped to this contract, so records from another of the building's contracts and visits you logged with **Record past visits** both appear. |
| **Invoices** | Every [invoice](/admin/billing/overview) raised off this contract. |

### The summary strip

| Card | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Lifts covered** | Total lifts, and how many coverage groups the contract is built from. |
| **Contract Period** | Start and expiry months, plus how many months of the term have elapsed. |
| **Annual cost** / **Cost per invoice** | The total of the contract's invoice lines. Only shown when the contract invoices automatically. Reads **TBC** when the lines are priced from the catalogue at the time of invoicing rather than fixed now. |
| **Next certificate expiry** | The earliest [operating-certificate](/start/glossary) expiry among the covered lifts. Only shown when annual inspection is switched on. Turns amber inside the renewal window and red once lapsed. |
| **Next invoice** | The month the next annual invoice will be drafted. Annual billing only. |

## Billing cadences

| Cadence | When a draft invoice appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Per-job** | When you close a job the contract generated. One draft per job. |
| **Annual** | Once a year, at the end of the contract's start month. |
| **None** | Never. You raise invoices by hand from Billing. |

Invoices are always created as **drafts**. Someone still reviews, approves and sends them — see [Billing](/admin/billing/overview).

A job that belongs to no contract at all raises a draft invoice when it closes only if it was created by converting a quote.

<Note>
  A contract only invoices while it is **Active**. Once it is expired or archived, closing one of its jobs raises nothing.
</Note>

## How the automation is timed

All dates and times are **Singapore time**.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Visits are written up front | Every visit for the whole term is computed when the contract is created, and recomputed whenever you edit it. |
| Monthly coverage groups | Due every month of the term. |
| Quarterly coverage groups | Due every third month, counted from the contract's start month. |
| Preferred day | The day of the month you chose. Weekends and public holidays shift to the nearest workday. |
| Job creation | 14 days before the visit date. The job arrives already assigned and time-slotted. |
| Who is told | Creating a job notifies nobody. Scheduling it notifies every active user of the building manager's firm — the firm, not one named contact — by push and by email, and pushes to the assigned technician or team. Moving a job later runs the same path, and the message still reads "scheduled". The nightly contract run sends one digest email per firm instead of one per job. |
| Long visits | A visit that does not fit one working day continues on the following workdays. Each day can be moved separately. |
| Backdated contracts | Scheduling runs from the current month forward. Months before today never generate jobs. |
| Certificate renewals | One grouped draft quote per building, raised 1–12 months (3 by default) before the earliest covered lift's certificate expiry. Needs annual inspection switched on, and a building manager on the contract. |
| Expiry | Checked on the nightly run. Past the expiry date, the contract flips to Expired. |

## Rules the builder enforces

- A lift can belong to only one [coverage group](/start/glossary) on a contract. (The builder labels these groups "Record 1", "Record 2" on screen.)
- Every coverage group needs a [record template](/start/glossary) and at least one lift. Nothing selects a template for you, and the builder refuses to save a group without one.
- Each coverage group carries its own frequency. The contract's **Frequency** reads **Mixed** when they do not all match.
- A record template written for a single lift must be given exactly one lift.
- Every lift in a coverage group must belong to the contract's building.
- The contract end must be after the contract start.
- A contract needs exactly one assignee — one technician **or** one team, never both and never none.
- If your organisation bills building managers, the building must have one before a contract can be created.

## Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Admin | Technician only | Call Center only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View contracts, open a contract | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Create a contract | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit a contract | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Reschedule an upcoming visit | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Archive a contract | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Record past visits | Yes | Yes | No | No |

<Note>
  **Call Center** is the role's name exactly as the product spells it. A technician-only account is not shut out of the web dashboard: it signs in normally and **Maintenance Contracts** is in its sidebar, so a technician can look a contract up. The **No** answers above are restrictions on those individual actions, not on reaching the area. Somebody who also holds Owner or Admin gets the Owner or Admin answer.
</Note>

## Rescheduling limits

An upcoming visit is moved from the contract's **Upcoming Visits** tab. It cannot be dragged on the calendar, where it appears as a [planned job](/start/glossary): the calendar refuses with "This job hasn't been generated yet — it'll be scheduled automatically closer to the date."

A visit cannot be moved when:

- It has already become a job. Move the job instead, from the job.
- The new date is in the past.
- The new date is on or after the contract's expiry.
- The contract is not Active.
