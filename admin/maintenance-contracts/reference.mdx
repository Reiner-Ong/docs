Everything the Maintenance Contracts area holds, for looking up rather than reading through. For how to actually do things, start at the [overview](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview).

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Can it be edited? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active** | The contract is inside its term and producing visits, jobs and invoices. | Yes |
| **Expired** | The contract has passed its expiry date. It produces nothing further. Existing jobs and invoices are unaffected. | No |
| **Archived** | Someone ended the contract early. All future visits were deleted. | No |

A contract becomes **Expired** on its own, on the nightly run, once the expiry date has passed. **Archived** is always a deliberate action.

<Note>
  **Expiring soon** is a filter, not a status. It picks out **Active** contracts that are more than 85% of the way through their term. Those show an amber expiry date in the list.
</Note>

## The contracts list

### Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Contract** | The contract reference, in the form `BP-2026-4F91C2`. |
| **Building** | The building the contract covers. One contract, one building. |
| **Frequency** | **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Mixed** when its records do not all share a frequency. |
| **Lifts** | How many lifts the contract covers in total, across all its records. |
| **Assignee** | The technician or the team that works this contract's visits. |
| **Billing** | **Per job**, **Annual**, or **No invoicing**. |
| **Term** | A progress bar for the term, and the expiry month. Click the header to sort by expiry. |
| **Status** | Active, Expired or Archived. |

### Filters and search

- **Status buttons** — All, Active, Expiring soon, Expired, Archived. Each carries a count.
- **All frequencies** — narrow to Monthly, Quarterly or Mixed.
- **All assignees** — narrow to one technician or team.
- **Search contracts…** — matches the contract reference and the building name.

## The contract page

### Tabs

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The records, the schedule, the billing setup, certificate renewals, a year-by-year service-activity grid, and a short list of recent and upcoming visits. |
| **Upcoming Visits** | Every future visit the contract will produce, with **Reschedule** on the ones that are not jobs yet. |
| **Visit History** | Every [job](/admin/jobs) this contract has produced, with its status. |
| **Invoices** | Every [invoice](/admin/invoices-and-payments) raised off this contract. |

### The summary strip

| Card | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Lifts covered** | Total lifts, and how many records the contract is built from. |
| **Contract Period** | Start and expiry months, plus how many months of the term have elapsed. |
| **Annual cost** / **Cost per invoice** | The total of the contract's invoice lines. Only shown when the contract invoices automatically. Reads **TBC** when the lines are priced from the catalogue at the time of invoicing rather than fixed now. |
| **Next certificate expiry** | The earliest operating-certificate expiry among the covered lifts. Only shown when annual inspection is switched on. Turns amber inside the renewal window and red once lapsed. |
| **Next invoice** | The month the next annual invoice will be drafted. Annual billing only. |

## Billing cadences

| Cadence | When a draft invoice appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Per-job** | When you close a job the contract generated. One draft per job. |
| **Annual** | Once a year, at the end of the contract's start month. |
| **None** | Never. You raise invoices by hand from Billing. |

Invoices are always created as **drafts**. Someone still reviews, approves and sends them — see [Invoices & Payments](/admin/invoices-and-payments).

<Note>
  A contract only invoices while it is **Active**. Once it is expired or archived, closing one of its jobs raises nothing.
</Note>

## How the automation is timed

All dates and times are **Singapore time**.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Visits are written up front | Every visit for the whole term is computed when the contract is created, and recomputed whenever you edit it. |
| Monthly records | Due every month of the term. |
| Quarterly records | Due every third month, counted from the contract's start month. |
| Preferred day | The day of the month you chose. Weekends and public holidays shift to the nearest workday. |
| Job creation | 14 days before the visit date. The job arrives already assigned and time-slotted. |
| Long visits | A visit that does not fit one working day continues on the following workdays. Each day can be moved separately. |
| Backdated contracts | Scheduling runs from the current month forward. Months before today never generate jobs. |
| Certificate renewals | One grouped draft quote per building, raised 1–12 months (3 by default) before the earliest covered lift's certificate expiry. Needs annual inspection switched on, and a building manager on the contract. |
| Expiry | Checked on the nightly run. Past the expiry date, the contract flips to Expired. |

## Rules the builder enforces

- A lift can belong to only one record on a contract.
- Every record needs a record template and at least one lift.
- A record template written for a single lift must be given exactly one lift.
- Every lift on a record must belong to the contract's building.
- The contract end must be after the contract start.
- A contract needs exactly one assignee — one technician **or** one team, never both and never none.
- If your organisation bills building managers, the building must have one before a contract can be created.

| Action | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| View contracts, open a contract | Owner, Admin, Technician |
| Create a contract | Owner, Admin, Technician |

| Reschedule an upcoming visit | Owner, Admin, Technician |

<Note>
  Someone whose only role is Technician, or only Call Center, does not get the web dashboard's admin pages at all — Maintenance Contracts included. The roles above apply to people who can already reach the dashboard.
</Note>

## Rescheduling limits

A visit cannot be moved when:

- It has already become a job. Move the job instead, from the job.
- The new date is in the past.
- The new date is on or after the contract's expiry.
- The contract is not Active.
