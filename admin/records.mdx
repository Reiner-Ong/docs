Records live in **Drive**. This page is a signpost to the guide you need.

For what a record is, what it contains and how sign-off reaches the building manager, see [Records](/start/concepts#records) in How LiftAuth Works.

## Where records live

Every record is filed in Drive, under the building it belongs to, the moment the technician submits it. There is no separate Records list in the dashboard.

## One thing worth knowing before you start

**A record cannot be corrected.** It cannot be edited once it exists, by anyone. A signed record cannot even be deleted; an unsigned one can be deleted by an owner or admin, but never amended — and nothing can re-create it afterwards. If something is wrong, the visit has to be recorded again.
