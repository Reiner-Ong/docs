A record is the report a technician produces when they fill in a form on site and submit it. It holds the answer to each item, any photos and notes, and the signatures.

## Where records live

Records are not a separate list any more. Every record is filed automatically in **Drive**, under the building it belongs to, the moment the technician submits it.

## Two things that changed

**Signing links are single-use and expire.** Each link works once and lasts 7 days. Generating a new link does **not** cancel the earlier ones — any unused link still works until it expires.

**Records cannot be edited after signing.** If something is wrong, the visit has to be recorded again; the signed record stands as it was.
