# Portfolio

Portfolio is where you manage the <Tooltip tip="One property or site where lifts are installed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Buildings</Tooltip> your company maintains, the <Tooltip tip="The company or owner responsible for a building. They receive invoices and sign completed work." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Building Managers</Tooltip> responsible for them, their lifts, and reported <Tooltip tip="A fault or problem reported against one lift and tracked until it is closed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Issues</Tooltip>.

## How the records fit together

Start with the building. It is the shared home for everything at one site. A <Tooltip tip="A building-level service schedule that covers one or more lifts." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Maintenance Plan</Tooltip> is the building's service schedule. It can include one or more lifts at that building.

## Add or find a building

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Buildings">
    Select **Portfolio** from the left sidebar. The **Buildings** tab opens your list of buildings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the site">
    Search by building name, browse the list, or switch to **Map** to find a building by location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or add the building">
    Select a building row to see its details. To add a new site, select **Add building**, enter its name and confirmed address, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Turn on **Show archived** when you need to find a building that is no longer active.
</Tip>

## Add a Building Manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Building Managers">
    In Portfolio, select the **Building Managers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the record">
    Select **Add building manager**, then enter the company or owner's name and, if available, billing address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign it to a building">
    Open the building, then find its **Building Manager** section and assign the record. You can also choose a Building Manager while creating a new building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the customer when needed">
    To connect the customer to their own LiftAuth organisation, select **Send request** and enter their email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Creating a Building Manager record does not send an email or create an account. A <Tooltip tip="An email request that lets a customer connect their own LiftAuth organisation to your private Building Manager record." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">connection request</Tooltip> is the separate step that does that. The customer does not become a member of your team.

See [how to add a building manager](/admin/building-managers) for the full guide.

## Add a lift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the building">
    From the **Buildings** tab, select the building where the lift is installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Lift">
    In the **Lifts** section, select **Add Lift**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the lift">
    Add the lift's details and save. It now has its own history, jobs, and issues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [how to add a lift](/admin/lifts) for the full guide.

## Raise or manage an issue

Select the **Issues** tab to see reported faults across every lift in your Portfolio. Use the status and severity filters to narrow the list, then select an issue to see its details.

To report a new fault, select **Raise Issue** and choose the lift first. This lets technicians see known faults when they arrive for a job.

See [how to raise and manage an issue](/admin/issues) for severity levels, photos, and closing an issue.
