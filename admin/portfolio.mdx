# Portfolio

Portfolio is where you manage the <Tooltip tip="One property or site where lifts are installed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Buildings</Tooltip> your company maintains, the <Tooltip tip="The company or owner responsible for a building. They receive invoices and sign completed work." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Building Managers</Tooltip> responsible for them, their lifts, and reported <Tooltip tip="A fault or problem reported against one lift and tracked until it is closed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Issues</Tooltip>.

## How the records fit together

Start with the building. It is the shared home for everything at one site. A <Tooltip tip="A building-level service schedule that covers one or more lifts." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Maintenance Plan</Tooltip> is the building's service schedule. It can include one or more lifts at that building.

## Find or add a building

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Buildings">
    Select **Portfolio** from the left sidebar. The **Buildings** tab opens your list of buildings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the site">
    Search by building name, browse the list, or switch to **Map** to browse buildings by location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or add the building">
    Select a building row to see its details. To add a new site, select **Add building**, enter its name and address, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Turn on **Show archived** when you need to find a building that is no longer active.
</Tip>
