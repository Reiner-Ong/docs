# Add a Building Manager

Use this guide when you need to add the property company or owner responsible for one or more buildings.

A <Tooltip tip="The company or owner responsible for a building. They receive invoices and sign completed work." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Building Manager</Tooltip> record is private to your LiftAuth company. Creating it does not email the customer, create an account for them, or add them to your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Portfolio">
    In the left sidebar, select <Tooltip tip="The dashboard area where your LiftAuth company keeps its buildings, Building Manager records, lifts, and issues together." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Portfolio</Tooltip>, then open the **Building Managers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new record">
    Select **Add building manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the record details">
    Enter the Building Manager's **Name**. Add a **Billing address** if you have it; this is the address invoices should use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Select **Add building manager**. The record appears in your Building Managers list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign the record to a building

Open the building in **Portfolio** and assign the Building Manager from the building details. One Building Manager record can be assigned to more than one of your buildings.

## Ask the customer to connect

Creating a record and contacting the customer are separate actions.

If the customer has their own LiftAuth organisation and should be able to connect it to this record:

1. Open the Building Manager record.
2. Select **Send request**.
3. Enter the customer's email address and send the <Tooltip tip="An email request that lets a customer connect their own LiftAuth organisation to your private Building Manager record." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">connection request</Tooltip>.

The customer chooses or creates their own organisation and accepts the request. They are not added to your team.

For the wider relationship between buildings, lifts, and issues, see [manage your portfolio](/admin/portfolio).
