A **record template** is the form a technician fills in on their phone during a
job. It decides what they are asked, in what order, and what ends up on the
[record](/admin/records) the building manager signs.

You find them under **Record Templates** in the left sidebar, in the
**Operations** group.

***

## How a template becomes a record

<Steps>
  <Step title="You build the template">
    You add sections and items — things like *Machine room clean*, *Door
    operation*, *Rope tension reading*. Each item has a type that decides how
    the technician answers it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A job uses the template">
    A maintenance job uses the template you chose for that lift on the
    building's maintenance contract. A repair or breakdown job uses your single
    repair or breakdown template automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The technician fills it in">
    In the mobile app the technician works through your items, adds remarks and
    photos where needed, and signs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="LiftAuth produces the record">
    The answers become a [record](/admin/records), which the building manager
    signs and which can be downloaded as a PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A template is a *mould*, not a copy. Every job stamps out a fresh record from
it, and each record keeps its own answers forever — even if you later change the
template.

## How many templates you need

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Maintenance — as many as you like" icon="calendar-check">
    Different lifts need different checks. Have one template for traction lifts,
    another for hydraulics, another for escalators. You choose which one applies
    to which lifts when you set up the building's maintenance contract.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Repair — exactly one" icon="badge-alert">
    Every repair job in your workspace uses the same template.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Breakdown — exactly one" icon="badge-x">
    Every breakdown call-out uses the same template.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

When your workspace was created, LiftAuth gave you a starter set — a Singapore
maintenance checklist plus a blank repair and a blank breakdown template. They
are yours: edit them freely.

***

## Getting started quickly

If you have never opened this page before, the shortest useful path is:

1. Open **Record Templates** and click into the maintenance template you were
   given.
2. Rename it to something your team recognises, and adjust the items to match
   how you actually service a lift.
3. Set **Estimated time** at the bottom to how long one of these takes — it is
   what LiftAuth uses to work out how long a site visit should be.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Full instructions are in
[How to create a record template](/admin/record-templates/creating-a-record-template).

<Note>
  Only **Owners** and **Admins** can create or change record templates.
  Technicians fill them in but never see this page.
</Note>
