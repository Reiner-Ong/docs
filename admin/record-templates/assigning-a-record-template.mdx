title: "How to assign a record template to lifts"
description: "Choose which maintenance template each lift is serviced against, using the building's maintenance contract."

A maintenance template does nothing until you say which lifts it covers. You do
that on the building's **maintenance contract**, which is what generates the recurring maintenance visits.

You need to be an **Owner** or an **Admin**.

On the contract, each card labelled **Record** is a [coverage group](/start/glossary#work-on-site) — one template, one frequency, and the lifts it covers. Naming the template here is the one place the choice is made for recurring visits: each generated job inherits it as a snapshot stamped at generation time. It is not the signed report of the same name: those are produced later, one for each form a technician submits.

<Note>
  Repair and Breakdown templates are **not** assigned in advance, and they are not applied for you either. Whoever schedules a repair or breakdown job picks the template on each form the job carries. This guide is only about maintenance, where the contract makes that choice once for the recurring visits.
</Note>

***

## Before you start

- The template exists and is not archived. Archived templates are not offered.
- The building and its lifts are already in LiftAuth. See
  [Adding Lifts](/admin/lifts).

***

## Assign a template on a new contract

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Maintenance Contracts">
    Click **Maintenance Contracts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click + New Contract">
    Pick the building in the **New maintenance contract** dialog, then click
    **Continue**. The setup wizard opens on the **Records** step, headed **Build
    your contract**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the record">
    The first card is headed **Record 1**. In the **Record** dropdown
    (*Select a record…*) pick your maintenance template. The help line under it
    reads *What the technician fills in for this record*.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the frequency">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Quarterly**. This is how often LiftAuth generates a
    maintenance record for each lift covered by this card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the lifts it covers">
    The lift list appears once a template is picked — before that you see
    *Pick a record above, then choose which lifts it covers*.

    - A **Single lift** template shows **Which lift?** and lets you pick one.
    - A **Multi-lift** template shows **Lifts in this record** and lets you tick
      several — one record covers them all.

    A lift already claimed by another card is greyed out and marked **In another
    record**.

    <Note>
      Templates you build yourself are always single-lift. Multi-lift only
      applies to certain forms added from the library, such as the BCA annual
      testing forms that list every lift in one table.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add more cards if the building needs them">
    Click **+ Add record** when different lifts in the same building need
    different templates or different frequencies — for example a monthly
    traction template for the passenger lifts and a quarterly escalator template
    for the escalators.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the wizard">
    Continue through **Recurring billing**, **Annual inspection**, **Schedule** and **Review**, then save. See [Maintenance Contracts](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview) for the rest of the contract.
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

## Change the template on an existing contract

1. Open **Maintenance Contracts** and click the contract.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. On the **Records** step, change the **Record** dropdown on the card you want.
4. Save.

Changes apply to jobs generated from now on. Jobs that already exist keep the
template they were created with.

***

## What LiftAuth checks before it lets you save

| Problem | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| No cards at all | *Add at least one record.* |
| A card with no template chosen | *Record 2: choose a record.* |
| A card with no lifts ticked | *Record 2: assign at least one lift.* |
| No maintenance templates exist yet | *No records to choose from yet. Create one under Record Templates first.* |

A lift left off every card is **allowed** — LiftAuth just tells you so:
*Not in any record: Lift 3. That's fine if it's serviced under another contract —
otherwise assign it to a record above.*

<Warning>
  A lift with no template on the contract still gets a maintenance job, but the technician receives a notes-only form instead of yours — free text and photos, with no [items](/start/glossary#work-on-site). If you expected a proper form, check the lift is ticked on one of the cards.
</Warning>

***

## How the template affects the visit length

Each template carries an **Estimated time per form**. On the **Schedule** step,
LiftAuth adds it up — one form per lift for a single-lift template, one form per
card for a multi-lift template — and rounds up to the nearest 15 minutes to
pre-fill **Estimated visit duration**.

You can override that figure on the contract. The hint **How the visit duration
works** shows the breakdown behind the auto-estimate, and explains that a visit
longer than the working day is split across consecutive working days.

<Tip>
  If your visits are consistently over- or under-booked, fix the template's
  estimate rather than overriding every contract — the template feeds all of
  them.
</Tip>
