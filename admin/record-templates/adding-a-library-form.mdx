The LiftAuth **library** is a set of ready-made forms maintained centrally —
mostly the Singapore BCA forms (permit applications, examination certificates,
notification forms, annual testing reports and inspection reports). Adding one
saves you retyping a regulator form, and keeps it correct when the regulator
changes it.

You need to be an **Owner** or an **Admin**.

<Note>
  The library is switched on per workspace. If the **Library forms** section is
  missing from the Record Templates page, it is off for you — contact LiftAuth
  support.
</Note>

***

## Add a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Record Templates">
    Click **Record Templates** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the Library forms section">
    Scroll past the Maintenance, Repair and Breakdown sections.

    If you have no library forms yet, the section shows **No library forms yet**
    with an **Add template from our library** button. If you already have some,
    use the **Add from library** button in the section header.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a form">
    The **Add from library** dialog groups forms by category — for example
    **Singapore · BCA**. Each row shows the form's name, its job type, how many
    items it has, and an **Overlay PDF** tag if it stamps onto the official PDF
    layout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add">
    The form appears in your **Library forms** section straight away, with a blue
    **Library** badge. A form you have already added shows **Added** instead of
    the button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

## What a library form is, and is not

A library form is a **live reference**, not a copy. Its contents are managed by
LiftAuth and stay up to date automatically in every workspace.

That means:

- It is **read-only**. You cannot rename it, change its items, or move its PDF
  boxes. Opening it shows *From the library · centrally managed*.
- Its estimated time comes from the library too.
- It is listed separately from the templates you authored, so it is obvious at a
  glance which forms you control.

<Warning>
  You cannot make an editable copy of a library form — **Duplicate** is not
  offered on one. If you need a variation, build it yourself with
  [How to create a record template](/admin/record-templates/creating-a-record-template).
</Warning>

***

## Remove a form you no longer need

1. Open the library form from the **Library forms** section.
2. Click **Remove from workspace**.
3. Confirm with **Remove**.

Removing it takes it off your pickers. It stays in the library, you can re-add it
at any time, and **existing jobs and records that already use it are
unaffected**.

***

## Why some forms look different from your own

An **Overlay PDF** form stamps the technician's answers onto the regulator's own
PDF layout, so the finished document looks exactly like the official form rather
than a LiftAuth-styled record. Your own templates use the auto-generated layout
unless you upload a PDF — see
[PDF output](/admin/record-templates/reference#pdf-output).
