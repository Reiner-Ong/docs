---
title: "How to archive or restore a record template"
description: "Retire a template you no longer use, without losing the records that were made from it."
icon: "archive"
---

Record templates are never deleted. Every job and record points back at the
template it came from, so LiftAuth retires a template by **archiving** it — it
disappears from the pickers, but stays resolvable forever.

You need to be an **Owner** or an **Admin**.

***

## Archive a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template">
    Go to **Record Templates** and click the template you want to retire.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Archive">
    The red **Archive** button is at the top right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read what it will do, then confirm">
    The dialog is headed **Archive maintenance template** (or repair, or
    breakdown) and explains that archiving hides the template from new
    record-template pickers, that existing jobs and records are unaffected, and
    that you can restore it at any time.

    Click **Archive record template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

## If a contract still uses it

You cannot archive a template that a maintenance contract still schedules. The
dialog shows:

> A building plan still schedules this record template. Re-point those plans
> before archiving.

To clear it:

1. Find the maintenance contracts that use the template. Open **Maintenance
   Contracts** and check each contract's records.
2. On each one, click **Edit**, change the **Record** dropdown to a different
   template, and save. See
   [How to assign a record template to lifts](/admin/record-templates/assigning-a-record-template).
3. Come back and archive.

***

## Where archived templates go

They move to an **Archived** section at the bottom of the Record Templates page,
each with a grey **Archived** badge. Nothing is lost — you can still open one and
read it.

***

## Restore a template

1. Open **Record Templates** and scroll to **Archived**.
2. Click the template.
3. Click **Restore**.

Restoring can fail if the slot has been taken while it was archived — either an
active template of the same name in the same job type, or, for Repair and
Breakdown, a second template of that job type. Rename or archive the conflicting
template first.

<Tip>
  This is the way to swap your single Repair or Breakdown template for a new one:
  archive the old one, which frees the slot, then create the replacement.
</Tip>

***

## Removing a library form instead

A form added from the library is not archived — it is removed. The button reads
**Remove from workspace** and the form goes back to being available in the
library. See
[How to add a form from the library](/admin/record-templates/adding-a-library-form#remove-a-form-you-no-longer-need).
