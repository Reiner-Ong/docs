title: "How to set the on-call schedule"
description: "Say which technician is on standby out of hours, so emergency call-outs reach the right person."
icon: "phone-volume"
---

Standby shifts are managed in **Dispatch**, on its **On call** tab — not on the Calendar page. They use the same calendar shell as the Calendar, which is why the rota looks and behaves like it, but the shifts themselves belong to Dispatch because that is where they are used: they rank the technician list when an operator raises an emergency call-out.
