The **on-call schedule** is a shared rota of standby shifts. It answers one
question: *who do we ring right now?* When an emergency call comes in, the
technicians who are on call are the ones offered first.

It lives on its own tab: click **Dispatch** in the left sidebar, then the
**On call** tab. It looks and works like the [Calendar](/admin/calendar/overview)
— the same month picker, the same Day / Week / Month / Agenda views — but it
shows exactly one thing: standby shifts, drawn as solid red bars labelled with
the technician's name.

<Note>
  Everyone in your organisation can read the rota, including technicians in the
  mobile app. Only owners and admins can change it.
</Note>

## Add a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the On call tab">
    **Dispatch** in the sidebar, then **On call**. It opens on **Month** — the
    view that answers "is next month covered?".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click ＋ Add on-call shift">
    You can also click an empty hour in Day or Week view, or **+ Add to this
    day** in the panel that opens when you click a date in Month view. Either
    way the dialog opens with that date and time already filled in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the technician">
    The list holds every active technician.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Starts and Ends">
    A shift defaults to **18:00 today → 08:00 tomorrow**, the usual overnight
    cover. A shift may cross midnight, and may run up to **7 days**; longer than
    that is rejected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set it to repeat (optional)">
    **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**, ending on the **Until** date. One
    entry then covers a standing rota such as "every Monday night". A monthly
    series repeats on the same day number each month, and skips a month that
    does not have that day.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add shift">
    The shift appears on the rota straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See who is covering

- The **Technicians** list on the left filters the rota. Untick anyone you do
  not want to see; the count underneath reads "4 of 6 shown". Your choice is
  remembered in this browser, and a technician who joins later starts out
  visible.
- Click a red bar to see the shift's date, time, and — if it repeats — its
  repeat rule.
- **Month** is the coverage view: gaps are the empty days. **Agenda** lists the
  month's shifts in order. Where nothing is scheduled you will see
  "No standby cover".

<Warning>
  LiftAuth does not warn you about gaps. Nothing flags an uncovered night, so
  check the month before it starts.
</Warning>

## Remove a shift

Click the red bar, then **Delete** on its card, then confirm:

- A one-off shift offers a single **Delete**.
- A repeating shift offers **This event** — removing only the night you clicked
  — and **All events**, which removes the whole series.

Shifts cannot be dragged, and one occurrence of a repeating shift cannot be
retimed. To change a shift, delete it and add the replacement.

## What the technician sees

The rota is read-only in the technician app:

- **Profile → ON CALL** shows an **On call** toggle and a link to the
  **On-call schedule**, with a line telling them either that they are on call
  now and until when, or when their next shift starts.
- The schedule screen shows the whole team's month, with the technician's own
  nights marked **You**.
- While they are on call, a blue **On call** banner sits at the bottom of the
  app.

The toggle is a manual override, for the real world: a technician who is not
scheduled can put themselves on call, and one who is scheduled can take
themselves off. **The most recent thing that happened wins** — so a technician
who switches off at 21:00 stays off for the rest of that shift, and comes back
on when their next shift begins.
