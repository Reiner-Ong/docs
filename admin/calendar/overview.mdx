The **Calendar** shows what your team is doing on any given day. Open it from
**Calendar** in the left sidebar, under *Operations*.

It is not a separate place where work is stored. It is a view over work that
already exists elsewhere in LiftAuth, laid out by date.

## What the calendar is made of

Everything you see on the calendar is one of three things:

| What you see | Where it comes from | Can you change it here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Job** | A real [job](/start/glossary) that has been scheduled to a person or a team | You can drag it to a new date while it is still _Scheduled_, assigned to one person rather than a team, and short enough to fit inside a single day. Everything else is edited on the job |
| **Planned job** | A visit a <Tooltip tip="Your servicing agreement with a building: which lifts are covered, how often they are visited, which forms are used, and how the work is billed." cta="Maintenance Contract" href="/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview">Maintenance Contract</Tooltip> will create later. It does not exist as a job yet | No — it is a preview |
| **Event** | Something a person typed straight onto their own calendar: leave, a meeting, a training day | Yes — create, edit and delete it here |

<Note>
  The calendar never creates jobs. A job is created from **Jobs**, from a Maintenance Contract, or from a quote. See [Jobs](/admin/jobs/overview).
</Note>

## One calendar per person

Every member of your team has their own calendar. The page starts by showing
**yours** and nobody else's. You add other people to the view by ticking them in
the **People** list on the left, and each person gets their own colour — the
same colour as their tick box — so an overlaid week stays readable.

Because it is a per-person view, a job only appears on the calendar of the
person (or the team) it is assigned to. An unassigned or unscheduled job appears
on nobody's calendar.
