---
title: "How to add, edit and delete an event"
description: "Block out leave, a meeting or a training day on a person's calendar — once or repeating."
icon: "calendar-plus"
---

An **event** is anything that takes up someone's time but is not a service
visit: annual leave, medical leave, a team meeting, a training day. Events live
only on the calendar. They do not create jobs and they do not stop anyone being
assigned work — they are there so the person planning the week can see the whole
picture.

## Add an event

There are three ways in, and they all open the same dialog:

- the **+ Add event** button at the top of the page,
- clicking an empty hour in the Day or Week view,
- **+ Add to this day** in the day panel that opens when you click a date in
  Month view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose whose calendar it goes on">
    The **Calendar** field appears only if you are an owner or admin and more
    than one person is ticked in the People list. Everyone else creates events
    on their own calendar, and the field is hidden.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it a title">
    Required. Keep it recognisable at a glance in a month cell — "Annual leave",
    "Toolbox meeting".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add notes (optional)">
    Free text. Notes show on the item's card when someone clicks it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set when it happens">
    Tick **All day** for something that has no clock time, such as leave.
    Otherwise set a start date and time and an end date and time. The end must
    be after the start, or the dialog will say so.

    An event may run over several days — set a later end date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set it to repeat (optional)">
    See below. Leave it on **Does not repeat** for a one-off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Create">
    The event appears immediately on the calendar it was created on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make an event repeat

Open the **Repeat** dropdown and pick **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. Two
more settings appear:

- **Every** — the interval. "Weekly, every 2" is fortnightly.
- **On days** — weekly only. Pick the weekdays it lands on.
- **Ends** — **Never**, **On** a date, or **After** a number of occurrences.

<Note>
  A repeating event is stored once and drawn on every date it lands on. Editing
  or deleting one of those dates does not touch the rest unless you say so.
</Note>

## Edit an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the event, then click Edit">
    The card that opens has **Edit** at the bottom.
  </Step>

  <Step title="If it repeats, choose what you are editing">
    Two buttons appear at the top of the dialog: **This event only** or
    **All events**. "This event only" changes just the date you clicked;
    "All events" changes the whole series, including its repeat rule.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change what you need, then click Save">
    You cannot move an event to a different person's calendar. Delete it and
    create it again on the right calendar instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete an event

Click the event and then **Delete** on its card. LiftAuth asks you to confirm,
because this cannot be undone:

- A one-off event offers a single **Delete**.
- A repeating event offers **This event** (removes only the date you clicked)
  and **All events** (removes the whole series).

<Warning>
  Only jobs and events can be removed this way. A **planned job** belongs to a
  [Maintenance Contract](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview) and has no delete button — change
  the contract if the visit should not happen.
</Warning>
