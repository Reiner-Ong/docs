title: "How to move a job or event by dragging"
description: "Drag an item to a new day or time slot on the calendar and confirm the move."

---

The quickest way to move a visit is to drag it. This is the same reschedule that
the job page performs — the technician's app and the people who need to know are
updated either way.

## Drag it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item in the view that suits the move">
    Use **Month** to move something to a different day; the time of day is kept
    as it was. Use **Week** or **Day** to change the time as well — the drop
    snaps to the nearest 15 minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drag the block onto the new day or slot">
    Nothing has changed yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the confirmation">
    A dialog opens headed **Reschedule job?** or **Reschedule event?**. It shows
    the new date and time, and the old one underneath so you can compare. If you
    dragged one date of a repeating event, it also tells you that only that
    occurrence will move.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Confirm">
    LiftAuth confirms with "Rescheduled. Relevant parties will be notified." The
    length of the visit is preserved — a two-hour job stays two hours long.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Cancel** at any point and nothing is changed.

## When a job will not move

Events always move. A job only moves while it is still **Scheduled**. If it
cannot be moved, LiftAuth says why as soon as you drop it, and the job stays
where it was:

| What you dragged | What LiftAuth tells you |
| --- | --- |
| A job that has not been scheduled yet | This job isn't scheduled yet — schedule it from the job first. |
| A job the technician has already finished | Work is already done on this job, so it can't be rescheduled. |
| A job the building manager has signed | This job is signed off, so it can't be rescheduled. |
| A closed job | This job is closed, so it can't be rescheduled. |
| A cancelled job | This job is cancelled, so it can't be rescheduled. |
| One day of a visit that spans several days | This visit spans several days — reschedule it from the job page. |
| A job assigned to a team rather than one person | This job is assigned to a team — reschedule it from the job page. |
| A planned job from a Maintenance Contract | This job hasn't been generated yet — it'll be scheduled automatically closer to the date. |

<Tip>
  Where dragging is refused, the job page can still do the job. Click the item, click **Open job**, and use **Reschedule** there — that is also where you change the technician, which dragging never does. See [Jobs reference](/admin/jobs/jobs-reference).
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Dragging keeps the same technician. To hand the visit to someone else, open
  the job and reschedule it there.
</Warning>
