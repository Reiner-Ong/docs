description: "Put people on the calendar, switch views, and open an item to see the job behind it."

Use this when you need to answer "who is free on Thursday?", "what is Daniel
doing next week?", or "is that visit still on?".

## Put people on the calendar

The **People** list runs down the left side of the page. It lists every active
team member who can be given work — owners, admins and technicians. You are at
the top, marked **(you)**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the people you want to see">
    Each person you tick is added to the grid in their own colour. The tick box
    is that colour, so you can read the grid without a separate legend.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Untick anyone you don't need">
    The count at the bottom of the list — "3 selected" — tells you how many
    calendars are currently drawn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your selection is remembered in the browser you are using, per organisation. It
is back the next time you open the page. If someone is later removed from the
team, they quietly drop out of your selection.

<Warning>
  Only owners and admins can look at other people's calendars. If you hold only
  the technician role and you tick a colleague, the page shows an error instead
  of their events. Untick them to get your own calendar back.
</Warning>

## Choose a view

Four views sit at the top of the page, next to the date. They all show the same
items — only the shape changes.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Day">
    One column, midnight to midnight, on a 24-hour time grid. Best for checking
    a single busy day hour by hour.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Week">
    Monday to Sunday on the same time grid. All-day items sit in a strip above
    the hours. This is the view for spotting a free afternoon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Month">
    A Monday-first month grid. Each day shows up to two items and then a
    **+N more** link. Click any day to open a panel on the right listing
    everything on that day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Agenda">
    The whole period as one list, grouped by day and ordered by time. When you
    have several people ticked, each row also names whose calendar it came from.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Move through time with the **‹** and **›** arrows beside the date. They step by
one day, one week or one month, depending on the view you are in.

The small month picker above the People list is a shortcut: click any date in it
and the calendar jumps to the **Day** view for that date. Its own arrows page
the picker forward and back without moving the main view, so you can look ahead
before you commit.

## Open an item to see what it is

Click any item — a job block, a planned visit, an event — and a small card opens
next to it. Depending on what you clicked, it shows:

- the date and time,
- **Day 2 of 3** when a job's visit spans several working days,
- the building and the lifts involved,
- the technician the work belongs to,
- for an event: whether it repeats, whose calendar it is on, and any notes.

The buttons at the bottom of the card take you to the real thing:

| Item | Button | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | **Open job** | The job page, where you can reschedule, cancel or close it |
| Planned job | **View plan** | The Maintenance Contract that will create the visit |
| Event | **Edit** / **Delete** | The event dialog, or the delete confirmation |

<Tip>
  A planned job is deliberately greyed out and cannot be dragged. LiftAuth
  creates the real job automatically closer to the date. If you need it to
  happen sooner or on a different day, change the
  [Maintenance Contract](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview) instead.
</Tip>
