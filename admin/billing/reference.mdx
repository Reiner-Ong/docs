A lookup page. For how to do things, start at the [Billing overview](/admin/billing/overview).

## The Billing tabs

| Tab | What it holds | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ledger** | Every issued quote, invoice, payment and credit note, newest first | Everyone who can open Billing |
| **Statements** | Per-customer statements of account and the ageing report | Owner, admin |
| **Standard items** | Your catalogue of repeat charges | Owner, admin |
| **Payments setup** | Stripe onboarding and payout status | Owner, admin — and only when online payments is enabled for your workspace |

Two more billing settings live on **Account → Billing setup**: *Payment settings* (the same Stripe page) and *[Billing reference numbers](/admin/billing/reference-numbers)*.

## The ledger

### Columns

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Reference** | Your own document number. Falls back to the internal handle on documents raised before numbering. |
| **Type** | Quote, Invoice, Payment, Credit note — or **Deposit** for a payment taken against a quote |
| **Status** | See the tables below. Overdue invoices show **Overdue** in place of their status. |
| **Customer** | The building manager |
| **Date** | Quote: sent date. Invoice: invoice date. Payment: date received. Credit note: date raised. |
| **Amount** | Signed — payments and credit notes are negative, because they reduce what is owed |

### Filters

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** segments | All / Quotes / Invoices / Payments / Credit notes, each with a count |
| **Show archived** | Off by default. Only quotes and invoices can be archived. |
| **Customer** | One building manager, or **All customers** |

Rows are paged 20 at a time.

### Row actions

| Action | Offered on |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | Every row |
| **Download PDF** | Quotes, invoices and credit notes. Payments have no PDF — a payment is an event, not a document your firm issues. |
| **Record payment** | Invoices with money still owed |

<Note>
  **Drafts are not on the ledger.** A draft quote or invoice is unfinished paperwork, not a transaction. Find drafts in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox).
</Note>

### Why the ledger sometimes says it is showing a subset

The ledger loads at most 500 of each document type. Past that, a line above the table says how many of how many you are looking at, and the counts, totals and figures describe that page only. Filter to one customer to see the rest of their account.

## The figures above the ledger

They describe the rows currently loaded, for the customer filtered to, and they always exclude archived documents.

| Figure | Counts | Excludes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open pipeline** | Quotes with status **Sent** | Drafts (not offered yet) and accepted quotes (already becoming invoices) |
| **Outstanding** | The unpaid balance of every issued invoice, plus a count of how many are overdue | Drafts, rejected, void |
| **Collected · 30d** | Settled payments received in the last 30 days, **net of refunds** | Processing, initiated and failed payments |
| **Credit outstanding** | Credit-note value not yet applied to an invoice | Void credit notes |

If the loaded documents are in more than one currency, the money figures show **—** rather than adding unlike currencies together.

## Quotes

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being written. No number, no PDF, not on the ledger. |
| **Sent** | Issued, numbered, signed, and with the customer. |
| **Accepted** | The customer committed to the price. |
| **Declined** | The customer said no. |
| **Expired** | The **Valid until** date passed with no decision. |
| **Superseded** | Replaced by a later revision. |
| **Converted** | A repair job was raised from it. |
| **Archived** | Filed away. Shown only when **Show archived** is on. |

### Actions by status

Everything below is available to owners and admins. The right-hand column flags
the four quote actions that owners and admins keep to themselves even where a
technician can otherwise work on the quote (see [Who can do what](#who-can-do-what)).

| Action | Available on | Owner/admin only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edit draft | Draft | |
| Discard draft | Draft | |
| Send quotation | Draft | |
| Record acceptance / Record decline | Sent, and only before **Valid until** passes | Yes |
| Email quotation / Email again | Sent | |
| Revise | Sent, Declined, Expired | Yes |
| Convert to repair job | Accepted | Yes |
| Create invoice | Accepted, Converted | Yes |
| Download PDF | Any, archived included | |
| Archive | Declined, Expired, Superseded | Yes |

Sending requires the sender to have a saved signature — LiftAuth prompts for one if there is none. Converting requires a technician or team to assign the job to.

An archived quote offers **Download PDF** and nothing else.

## Invoices

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being written. No number, not on the ledger, not owed. | You |
| **Issued** | Billed. Numbered, sealed, counted as owed. | You, by issuing |
| **Part paid** | Some of the balance settled | LiftAuth, from the ledger |
| **Paid** | Balance is zero | LiftAuth, from the ledger |
| **Rejected** | A draft that was discarded | You |
| **Void** | Cancelled | Not currently produced by the product — reverse an issued invoice with a credit note instead |
| **Archived** | Filed away. Only a draft or rejected invoice can be archived. | You |

**Overdue** is not a status. It is derived: an **Issued** or **Part paid** invoice with a balance above zero, past its due date. An invoice with no due date is never overdue.

**Balance** = invoice total − settled payments allocated to it − credit notes applied to it.

### Actions by status

All of these are owner and admin actions.

| Action | Available on |
| --- | --- |
| Edit draft | Draft |
| Issue invoice | Draft |
| Discard draft | Draft, Rejected |
| Email invoice / Email again | Issued, Part paid (once the PDF exists) |
| Record payment | Issued, Part paid |
| Raise credit note | Issued, Part paid, Paid |
| Download PDF | Any invoice with a PDF |

An invoice cannot be issued without at least one line item and payment terms. Once issued, the customer, lines, dates and terms are frozen permanently.

## Payments

### Statuses

| Status | Settles the invoice? |
| --- | --- |
| **Recorded** | Yes — a legacy or manual entry with no gateway status |
| **Succeeded** | Yes |
| **Processing** | No — started online, not yet cleared |
| **Failed** | No |
| **Refunded** | The full amount was handed back |
| **Part refunded** | Some of it was handed back |

### Methods

| Method | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual** | Recorded by hand |
| **Bank transfer** | Recorded by hand |
| **Card** / **PayNow** | Taken through [online payments](/admin/billing/online-payments); recorded automatically |

### Actions

Both are owner and admin actions.

| Action | Available when |
| --- | --- |
| Open invoice | The payment settles one |
| Delete payment | Not already deleted, not failed, not refunded |

Deleting is a correction of your own record, not a refund. The invoice's balance goes back up.

## Credit notes

### Kinds

| Kind | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Adjustment** | Correcting an over-bill |
| **Refund** | Money returned. Raised automatically when an online payment is refunded. |
| **Write-off** | Bad debt |

All three reduce the balance identically; the kind is for your records.

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Credit on the account, not yet applied to any invoice |
| **Applied** | Fully drawn down against one or more invoices |
| **Void** | Cancelled. Still visible and downloadable; credits nothing. |

A credit note raised against a specific invoice starts **Applied** — the full amount goes onto that invoice at once, capped at what it still owes. One raised against the customer with no invoice starts **Open**.

### Actions

All three are owner and admin actions — credit notes are owner/admin only throughout.

| Action | Available when |
| --- | --- |
| Apply to invoice | Not void, and credit is left to apply |
| Download PDF | Always |
| Void credit note | Not already void |

## Ageing buckets

Used by the Statements tab to sort and badge accounts.

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Current** | Not yet due, or up to 30 days past due. No badge. |
| **31–60 days** | 31 to 60 days past due |
| **61–90 days** | 61 to 90 days past due |
| **90+ days** | More than 90 days past due |

An account is badged by its **oldest** bucket holding money.

## Who can do what

Billing uses three roles.

| Role | Billing access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything |
| **Admin** | Everything |
| **Technician** | Someone whose *only* role is Technician cannot open the Billing area in the dashboard. They can still work with quotes and read invoices from the surfaces available to them: creating, editing, sending, emailing and downloading a quote, and reading and emailing an invoice. |

Narrower gates worth knowing:

- **Credit notes, statements, ageing, standard items and reference numbers** are owner and admin only — including reading them.
- **Accepting, declining, revising, converting and archiving a quote** are owner and admin only, even though a technician can create and send one.
- **Creating, editing, issuing and discarding an invoice**, and **recording or deleting a payment**, are owner and admin only.
- **Stripe Connect setup** is owner and admin only, and only on a servicing firm's workspace.
- **Standard items** is hidden as a tab rather than shown and refused, because its rows carry your cost prices.

## Things LiftAuth deliberately will not do

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Delete an invoice">
    Never. Once issued, an invoice is on record permanently so your billing history stays complete for compliance and audit. Reverse it with a credit note.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Edit an issued invoice or a sent quote">
    Never. The customer holds that document. Revise the quote, or credit the invoice and raise a corrected one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Allocate more to an invoice than it has outstanding">
    You cannot allocate more to an invoice than it has outstanding — a manual over-allocation is refused. A card payment that overshoots settles the invoice and leaves the surplus as unallocated customer credit, which you can apply to another invoice. A genuine refund is issued from the payment itself, which raises a refund credit note automatically — you do not write one by hand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email a statement">
    Not supported. Download the PDF and send it yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send an automatic overdue reminder">
    Not supported. Chase from the Statements tab and email the invoice again by hand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
