title: "How to accept online payments"
description: "Set up Stripe so building managers can pay your invoices by card or PayNow, and the payment reconciles itself."

---

With online payments switched on, a building manager can pay an issued invoice from their own LiftAuth app — by card or PayNow — and the payment lands against that invoice automatically. No bank statement to check, no payment to type in.

The money goes to **your** bank account. LiftAuth uses Stripe to move it, so you complete Stripe's verification once and Stripe pays out to you from then on.

<Note>
  Online payments are set up from a servicing firm's workspace only. If you do not see **Payments setup** as a tab in Billing, the feature is not enabled for your workspace — ask LiftAuth support to turn it on.
</Note>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing → Payments setup">
    You can also reach it from **Account → Billing setup → Payment settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Set up payments">
    Stripe opens in a new tab. If your browser blocks the tab, allow pop-ups for LiftAuth and click again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Stripe's verification">
    Stripe asks for your business details, identity documents and a bank account to pay out to. This is Stripe's own form, not LiftAuth's.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Come back to LiftAuth">
    The page updates itself. There is nothing to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The four states

The Payments setup page always tells you exactly where you are.

| State | What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Not started** | "Accept online payments" | Click **Set up payments** |
| **In progress** | "Finish setting up", badge **In progress** | Click **Continue setup** — Stripe still needs a few details |
| **Under review** | "Under review", badge **Verifying** | Wait. Stripe is checking your details — usually minutes. LiftAuth updates itself. |
| **Complete** | "Accepting online payments", badge **Active** | Nothing. You are live. |

## Once you are live

The page becomes your payment-account home and shows:

- **Methods** — Card and PayNow
- **Payouts** — whether Stripe has a bank account to pay you into. If it says *Add a bank account in Stripe*, payments will still be taken but nothing can be paid out to you until you add one.
- **Currency** — SGD
- **Stripe account** — your connected account's id

**Open payments dashboard** mints a one-time login into your own Stripe dashboard, where you manage your balance, payouts, transactions, disputes and refunds.

<Warning>
  The dashboard link is single-use. Click the button again each time rather than bookmarking the page it opens.
</Warning>

## What the building manager does

They open the invoice in their LiftAuth app and pay it. For them to see it at all, two things must be true:

1. They are **connected** to your firm in LiftAuth.
2. The invoice is **Issued** — a draft is invisible to them.

## What you see when they pay

The payment appears on your [ledger](/admin/billing/overview#finding-a-document) on its own. You do not record anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Processing">
    The payment has been started but has not cleared. It reduces nothing yet, and shows a **Processing** badge.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Succeeded">
    The money has cleared. The invoice's balance drops and its status moves to **Part paid** or **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A payment that does not go through shows as **Failed** and settles nothing — the invoice is exactly as it was.

<Note>
  A building manager who pays you by bank transfer instead may tap **I've made payment** in their app. That is only a nudge: it puts the invoice in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox) so you can check your bank and [record the payment](/admin/billing/payments) yourself.
</Note>

## Refunds

Refunds are made in your Stripe dashboard. There is no refund action anywhere in LiftAuth.

<Warning>
  A refund you make in Stripe is **not picked up by LiftAuth at all** — no credit note is raised, the payment's status does not change, and the invoice's balance stays where it was. Record the reversal yourself by raising a [credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes) of kind _Refund_ for the amount you refunded, or LiftAuth will go on treating the invoice as settled by money you have handed back.
</Warning>
