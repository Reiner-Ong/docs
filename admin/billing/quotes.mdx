A **quote** is a price you offer a building manager before the work is agreed. It commits nothing until they accept it. Once they do, you can turn it into a repair job, an invoice, or both.

## Raise the quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click **Billing** in the left sidebar, under Finance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click + New billing → Quote">
    The **+ New billing** button is at the top right. Choose **Quote** — "Price work before it is agreed".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    Pick the building manager the quote is for, and the building if it applies. If you had already filtered the ledger to one customer, that customer is filled in for you — you can change it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the line items">
    Each line has a description, a quantity and a unit price. Lines come from three places: an inventory part, one of your [standard billing items](/admin/billing/standard-items), or typed in by hand.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the terms">
    Currency, tax rate (a percentage — 9% is prefilled for GST), **Valid until**, payment terms, and any notes. Leave **Valid until** blank if the price does not expire.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    The quote is saved as a **draft**. Nothing has reached the customer yet, and the quote does not appear on the ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you quote for the same kind of job repeatedly, save the finished draft as a template. Next time, apply the template and the lines, tax rate, terms and notes are filled in for you.
</Tip>

### Asking for a deposit

A quote can carry a deposit — either a percentage of the total or a fixed amount. Set it in the **Deposit** section of the builder. A deposit you later collect is recorded as a payment against the quote rather than against an invoice, and shows on the ledger as a **Deposit** row.

## Send the quotation

Sending is what makes the quote real. It stamps the quote with its number, seals the PDF with your signature, and moves it to **Sent**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft">
    Find it in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox), or open the customer's account and pick it from there. Drafts are not on the ledger.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Send quotation">
    If you have never saved a signature, LiftAuth asks you to draw one first. No quotation is ever issued unsigned. Abandoning the signature abandons the send — the quote stays a draft.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the number">
    The confirmation shows the number the quote is about to take from your [quote series](/admin/billing/reference-numbers). You can type a different one; a number you type is used as-is and does not move the counter.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    The quote becomes **Sent** and appears on the ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Sending a quotation does not email it. If the building manager uses the LiftAuth app and is connected to you, the quotation appears there immediately. If they are not, nothing has reached them yet — [email it](/admin/billing/emailing-documents) as a separate step. The send card tells you which case you are in.
</Warning>

## Record what the customer decided

If the building manager accepts or declines in their own app, LiftAuth records it and the quote appears in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox). If they tell you by phone or email instead, record it yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sent quote from the ledger">
    Click the row. The quote opens in an overlay.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Record acceptance or Record decline">
    For an acceptance, enter who accepted it. For a decline, enter the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot record a decision after the **Valid until** date has passed. The quote shows a **Validity lapsed** badge and the two decision actions disappear. Use **Revise** to issue a fresh revision at a current price.
</Warning>

## What happens after acceptance

An accepted quote offers two next steps, and you can use either or both.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Convert to repair job" icon="wrench">
    Creates one [repair job](/admin/jobs/overview) from the quote, with the quoted parts already listed against it. You must name a technician or team to convert. A draft invoice is seeded from the quote when the job is completed — no invoice is created at conversion.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Create invoice" icon="file-invoice">
    Opens the invoice builder with the quote's line items already copied across. Use this when you want to bill now rather than on completion.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Changing a quote after it has gone out

You cannot edit a sent quotation — the customer is holding that document. Instead, click **Revise**. LiftAuth creates a new quote carrying the same lines, opens it in the editor, and marks the old one **Superseded** once the revision is sent. The revision number appears on the document and on the PDF filename.

Revise is available on a quote that is **Sent**, **Declined** or **Expired**. Only an owner or admin can revise.

## Filing a quote away

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discard a draft">
    Open the draft and click **Discard draft**. Nothing was sent, so nothing is lost from the customer's point of view.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Archive a finished quote">
    A **Declined**, **Expired** or **Superseded** quote can be archived, which hides it from the ledger unless **Show archived** is on. Archiving is one-way: an archived quote keeps its PDF and nothing else — no lifecycle action is offered on it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I archive this one?">
    A **Draft** is discarded rather than archived. A **Sent** quote is still with the customer, and an **Accepted** or **Converted** quote already has a job or an invoice descending from it — LiftAuth refuses all three.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Quote statuses at a glance

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being written. Not numbered, not on the ledger. |
| **Sent** | Issued and with the customer. Awaiting their decision. |
| **Accepted** | The customer committed to the price. |
| **Declined** | The customer said no. |
| **Expired** | The **Valid until** date passed with no decision. |
| **Superseded** | A later revision replaced it. |
| **Converted** | A job was raised from it. |

Full detail, including who can perform each action, is in [Billing reference](/admin/billing/reference#quotes).
