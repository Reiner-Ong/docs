title: "How to record a payment"
description: "Log money you have received and settle it against the right invoice."

A **payment** is money you have received. Recording it in LiftAuth is what reduces the customer's balance and moves the invoice to **Part paid** or **Paid**. Payments made online by card or PayNow record themselves — this page is for money that arrived any other way.

## Record it

There are three routes to the same form. Pick whichever is nearest.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="From the invoice" icon="file-invoice">
    Open the invoice from the ledger and click **Record payment**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From the ledger row" icon="list">
    Use the row's **⋯** menu → **Record payment**. Offered on any invoice with money still owed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From scratch" icon="plus">
    **+ New billing → Payment** — "Record money received".
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    Already filled in when you started from an invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the invoice">
    **Against invoice** lists that customer's open invoices. A payment is recorded against one invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Must be greater than zero, and cannot exceed the invoice's outstanding balance. To settle a balance exactly, enter the outstanding figure shown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the date received">
    **Received on** is the date the money actually landed, which may not be today.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the method">
    **Manual** or **Bank transfer**. Card and PayNow payments are not recorded here — they arrive on their own through [online payments](/admin/billing/online-payments).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a reference (optional)">
    Your bank's transaction reference, for example. It shows on the ledger row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    The payment lands on the ledger as a negative amount, and the invoice's status and balance update immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot allocate more to an invoice than it has outstanding — a manual over-allocation is refused, so record the payment up to the balance. A card payment that overshoots settles the invoice and leaves the surplus as unallocated customer credit, which you can apply to another invoice. A genuine refund is issued from the payment itself, which raises a refund [credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes) automatically — you do not write one by hand.
</Warning>

## Payment statuses

A payment you record by hand is settled the moment you save it — it shows as **Recorded** or **Succeeded**. The other statuses only appear on payments that came through the online payment gateway.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Recorded** | Entered by hand. Settled. |
| **Succeeded** | Cleared through the gateway. Settled. |
| **Processing** | Started online, not yet cleared. Does not reduce the balance. |
| **Failed** | The online payment did not go through. Settles nothing. |
| **Refunded** | The whole amount was handed back. |
| **Part refunded** | Some of it was handed back. |

Only settled money counts. A **Processing** or **Failed** payment leaves the invoice exactly as it was, and the **Collected · 30d** figure ignores both.

## Deposits

A deposit collected against a quote — before any invoice exists — appears on the ledger as a **Deposit** row rather than a Payment. It has no invoice to open, because there is not one yet.

## Correcting a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment from the ledger">
    Click the row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Delete payment">
    Confirm. The payment leaves the ledger and the invoice's balance goes back up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Delete is for correcting a mistake in your own record-keeping — an amount typed wrong, a payment logged against the wrong invoice. It is not a refund. To hand money back, refund the original payment — that raises a refund [credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes) automatically.
</Warning>

Delete is not offered on a **Failed** payment (it moved no money), on one already deleted, or on one that has been refunded — the refund's credit note points at it, and removing it would orphan that document.

## Refunding an online payment

There is no Refund button in the LiftAuth dashboard. Card and PayNow refunds are made in your Stripe dashboard — open it from **Billing → Payments setup → Open payments dashboard**.

When a refund goes through, LiftAuth keeps up on its own: the payment becomes **Refunded** or **Part refunded**, and a **credit note** of kind *Refund* is raised against the invoice automatically, so the customer's balance matches the money that actually moved.
