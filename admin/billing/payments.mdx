A **payment** is money you have received. Recording it in LiftAuth is what reduces the customer's balance and moves the invoice to **Part paid** or **Paid**. Payments made online by card or PayNow record themselves — this page is for money that arrived any other way.

## Record it

There are three routes to the same form. Pick whichever is nearest.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="From the invoice" icon="file-invoice">
    Open the invoice from the ledger and click **Record payment**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From the ledger row" icon="list">
    Use the row's **⋯** menu → **Record payment**. Offered on any invoice with money still owed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From scratch" icon="plus">
    **+ New billing → Payment** — "Record money received".
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    Already filled in when you started from an invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the invoice">
    **Against invoice** lists that customer's open invoices. A payment is recorded against one invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Must be greater than zero, and cannot exceed the invoice's outstanding balance. To settle a balance exactly, enter the outstanding figure shown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the date received">
    **Received on** is the date the money actually landed, which may not be today.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the method">
    **Manual** or **Bank transfer**. Card and PayNow payments are not recorded here — they arrive on their own through [online payments](/admin/billing/online-payments).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a reference (optional)">
    Your bank's transaction reference, for example. It shows on the ledger row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    The payment lands on the ledger as a negative amount, and the invoice's status and balance update immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot allocate more to an invoice than it has outstanding — a manual over-allocation is refused, so record the payment up to the balance. A card payment that overshoots settles the invoice and leaves the surplus showing as **Unallocated credit** on the customer's statement. There is no action that applies a payment surplus to another invoice: only a [credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes) can be applied.
</Warning>

## Payment statuses

A payment you record by hand is settled the moment you save it, and is stored as **Succeeded**. **Recorded** is only a fallback label for older rows that carry no status at all. The remaining statuses are produced by the online payment rails.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Succeeded** | Settled. This is what a hand-recorded payment gets, and what a cleared card or PayNow payment gets. |
| **Recorded** | Legacy fallback label for a row with no stored status. Settled. |
| **Processing** | Started online, not yet cleared. Does not reduce the balance. |
| **Failed** | The online payment did not go through. Settles nothing. |
| **Refunded** | The whole amount was handed back. |
| **Part refunded** | Some of it was handed back. |

Only settled money counts. A **Processing** or **Failed** payment leaves the invoice exactly as it was, and the **Collected · 30d** figure ignores both.

## Deposits

A deposit collected against a quote — before any invoice exists — appears on the ledger labelled **Deposit** rather than Payment. It has no invoice to open, because there is not one yet.

Underneath, a Deposit is still a payment, so the ledger's **Payments** filter (and **All**) returns it. Only the wording differs: the filter segment says Payments, the row says Deposit.

## Correcting a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment from the ledger">
    Click the row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Delete payment">
    Confirm. The payment leaves the ledger and the invoice's balance goes back up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Delete is for correcting a mistake in your own record-keeping — an amount typed wrong, a payment logged against the wrong invoice. It is not a refund, and there is no refund action in LiftAuth to reach for instead. To reverse an issued invoice, [raise a credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes).
</Warning>

Delete is not offered on a **Failed** payment (it moved no money), on one already deleted, or on one that has been refunded — a credit note may point at it, and removing it would orphan that document.

## Refunding money you have taken online

**There is no refund action anywhere in the LiftAuth dashboard.** Card and PayNow refunds are made in your Stripe dashboard — open it from **Billing → Payments setup → Open payments dashboard**.

<Warning>
  A refund you make in Stripe is **not picked up by LiftAuth at all**. No credit note is raised, the payment's status does not change, and the customer's balance and the ledger stay exactly as they were.

  So LiftAuth will go on showing the invoice as settled by money you have already handed back. Unless you record the reversal yourself — [raise a credit note](/admin/billing/credit-notes) of kind **Refund** for the amount you refunded — your LiftAuth figures will not reconcile with your bank or with Stripe.
</Warning>
