The **Statements** tab in Billing answers two questions: _which customers are behind?_ and _what exactly does this customer owe, and why?_

## Find the accounts that are late

Open **Billing → Statements**. The list of accounts down the left is sorted worst-first, so the ones that need chasing are already at the top. Each late account carries a badge naming how old its oldest unpaid money is.

There are four ageing bands, and only three of them are badged.

| Band | Meaning | Badge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current** | Not yet due, **or** up to 30 days past its due date | None |
| **31–60 days** | Oldest unpaid money is 31 to 60 days past its due date | **31–60 days** |
| **61–90 days** | 61 to 90 days past due | **61–90 days** |
| **90+ days** | More than 90 days past due | **90+ days** |

<Note>
  **Current** covers more than you might expect: money up to 30 days late is still Current and gets no badge. That is deliberate — the first month of lateness is the normal rhythm of a working account, and badging it would make the badge meaningless. The sort order still surfaces it.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The ledger and the ageing report disagree on purpose, and it is not a bug. An invoice one day past its due date gets a red **Overdue** badge on the [ledger](/admin/billing/overview#finding-a-document), while the ageing report here still counts it as **Current** with no badge. The ledger badge answers "is this invoice late?"; the ageing band answers "how late, and should I chase it?" The same invoice can therefore read **Overdue** in one place and **Current** in the other for its first 30 days.
</Warning>

An account's badge names its **oldest** money. A customer with both a fresh invoice and one from four months ago reads as **90+ days**, because that is the problem to deal with.

## Read a customer's statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing → Statements">
    The view starts on **By account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Use the search box above the list if you have many customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    Two date pickers, **From** and **To**. Anything before the start date is rolled into the opening balance rather than dropped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The statement is balance-forward: it starts from what the customer owed before the period, lists every movement inside it in date order, and ends at the closing balance.

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Opening** | Owed before the start date |
| **Invoiced** | Everything billed inside the period |
| **Settled** | Payments received inside the period |
| **Credits** | Credit notes applied inside the period |
| **Unallocated credit** | Open credit the customer holds that has not been applied to any invoice |
| **Closing balance** | Owed at the end of the period |

Every line names the document behind it. Click one to open that invoice, payment or credit note without leaving the statement.

<Tip>
  If a closing balance does not match what you expected, check **Unallocated credit**. Credit raised but never [applied to an invoice](/admin/billing/credit-notes#apply-an-open-credit-note) sits on the account without reducing any particular invoice.
</Tip>

## Reconcile every account at once

Switch the view from **By account** to **All accounts**. Every customer is stacked on one page for the same period, with a roll-up of opening balance, invoiced, payments, credits and closing balance across the whole book. This is the view that matches the exports line for line.

<Note>
  If your accounts are not all in the same currency, the roll-up figures show as **—**. Summing unlike currencies would give a wrong number wearing a currency symbol.
</Note>

## Export a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the period and, if you want one account only, select it">
    The export contains exactly what is on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download PDF or Download CSV">
    PDF for something to send or file; CSV for your accounting software.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  LiftAuth does not email statements. Download the PDF and attach it to your own email. (Quotes and invoices _can_ be emailed from LiftAuth — see [How to email a quote or invoice](/admin/billing/emailing-documents).)
</Warning>

If the start date is after the end date, LiftAuth says so and both export buttons stay disabled.

## What counts as overdue

An invoice is overdue when it is **Issued** or **Part paid**, still has a balance, and today is past its due date. Every invoice has a due date — it is required — so there is no invoice that can never fall overdue. Invoices with a zero balance are excluded from the ageing report entirely, as are void invoices (a status nothing in LiftAuth sets).

The ageing report is as-of _right now_; a statement is as-of the end date you chose. If the two disagree, that is why.

<Tip>
  A statement of account is the surface to use when you need a customer's complete history. The [ledger](/admin/billing/overview#finding-a-document) loads at most 500 documents of each type and its totals describe only what is loaded; a statement reads the whole account.
</Tip>
