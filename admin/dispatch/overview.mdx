**Dispatch** is where you handle an emergency call-out: someone phones to say a lift has failed or that people are trapped, and you ring your technicians' phones until one of them accepts the job of going.

It is a separate area from [Jobs](/admin/jobs/overview) because it works differently. A job is planned work you schedule for a date. A dispatch is happening right now, so instead of assigning one person and hoping they see it, LiftAuth makes several phones ring at once with a siren, and the first technician to tap **Accept dispatch** owns the call-out.

## The shape of a dispatch

<Steps>
  <Step title="A call comes in">
    An operator takes the phone call and raises the dispatch against the building the caller is at.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phones ring">
    Every technician the operator picked gets a loud ring on their phone. It keeps ringing until someone responds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="One technician accepts">
    The first tap wins. Everyone else's phone stops ringing and shows that the call-out has been taken.
  </Step>

  <Step title="They travel and arrive">
    The technician can share their live location on the way, then taps **I've arrived** when they are on site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="An operator closes it">
    Once the situation is handled, the operator closes the dispatch. That is the end of the record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A **call-out** is one emergency attendance; a **dispatch** is how LiftAuth finds a technician for it. A call-out is not a [job](/start/glossary), and the dispatch itself creates no job, no [record](/admin/records) and no invoice. If the attendance turns into billable work, someone raises a **separate breakdown job** for it in the normal way — the call-out and that job stay two different things. A dispatch is also raised against a **building**, never against a specific lift.
</Note>

Dispatch has two tabs:

* **Dispatch** — the live queue of call-outs, newest first, with the detail of whichever one you select.
* **On call** — the standby rota: who is rostered to take emergency calls, and when.

<Note>
  Dispatch is an optional area. If you do not see **Dispatch** in the left sidebar, it has not been switched on for your team — ask your workspace owner.
</Note>

## Reading the queue at a glance

Each row in the queue carries a coloured status badge and a timer, so you can see the state of every call-out without opening it.

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ringing** | Phones are ringing and nobody has accepted yet. A call-out also comes back to this badge if the technician who accepted releases it. |
| **Unanswered** | Still ringing after three minutes. Nobody has picked it up. |
| **On the way** | A technician accepted and is travelling. |
| **Arrived** | The technician has marked themselves on site. |
| **Stood down** | An operator cancelled the call-out. |
| **Closed** | An operator closed it after it was handled. |

The full list of statuses, timers and fields is on the [Dispatch reference](/admin/dispatch/reference) page.
