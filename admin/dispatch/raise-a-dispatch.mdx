Use this when someone phones in an emergency — a lift has stopped, or people are trapped inside one — and you need a technician on site now.

<Warning>
  Starting a dispatch makes real phones ring with a siren. Only start one for a genuine call-out.
</Warning>

## Before you start

You need the **Owner**, **Admin** or **Call Centre** role. You also need to know which building the caller is at — a dispatch is raised against a building, so if the caller cannot name it, ask for the address and search your buildings for it in the dashboard.

## Raise the dispatch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dispatch">
    Click **Dispatch** in the left sidebar. You land on the **Dispatch** tab, which shows the live queue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click New emergency dispatch">
    The red **＋ New emergency dispatch** button is at the top right. The form opens with the incident on the left and the technicians to ring on the right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the building">
    Type into **Building** and pick from the list. This is required — everything else on the left can be left blank if the caller is in a hurry.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Say whether anyone is trapped">
    Choose **Yes — entrapment** or **No one trapped**. If you choose entrapment, a counter appears — set how many people are inside. This is the single most important detail for the technician, so ask the caller before anything else.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the caller's details">
    Fill in **Caller name** and **Caller phone** if you have them. The phone number becomes a tap-to-call link for both you and the technician who accepts, so it is worth capturing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write down what was reported">
    Use **Situation** for the caller's own words — "doors won't open on level 4", "loud bang then it stopped". The technician reads this on their phone before they set off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the technicians to ring">
    On the right, tick everyone you want to ring. At least one is required. Nothing is pre-selected — you choose every time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Start dispatch — ring N**. The dialog closes, the new call-out appears at the top of the queue as **Ringing**, and the phones start.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing who to ring

The technician list is grouped to make the decision easy at 3am:

* **On call** — the people rostered on the [on-call rota](/admin/dispatch/on-call-rota) right now, or who have switched themselves on call in their app. This group is open by default, and each row shows the span they are on for, such as *from Mon 18:00 to Tue 08:00*.
* **Not on call** — everyone else, folded away behind one click.

The grouping only ranks people. Nobody is blocked: you can tick anyone in either group, and you should when the on-call technician is not answering or the site needs two pairs of hands.

<Tip>
  Ring more than one person. The first to tap **Accept** takes the call-out and everyone else's phone stops ringing automatically, so there is no cost to casting a wider net.
</Tip>

Only **active technicians** appear in the list. Office staff, call-centre operators, and anyone suspended or still pending an invitation are not shown, because a ring goes to the people who attend site. If the list is empty, nobody in your team holds the technician role — see [Manage your team](/admin/managing-your-team).

If you see **No one is on call right now**, the rota has nobody rostered for this moment. You can still ring anyone from the **Not on call** group.

## After you start

The call-out lands at the top of the queue with a red **Ringing** badge and a timer counting up. What to do next — and what to do if nobody answers — is covered in [Follow a live dispatch](/admin/dispatch/follow-a-live-dispatch).
