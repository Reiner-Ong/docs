Look-up detail for the [Dispatch](/admin/dispatch/overview) area. For how to actually do things, see [Raise an emergency dispatch](/admin/dispatch/raise-a-dispatch) and [Follow a live dispatch](/admin/dispatch/follow-a-live-dispatch).

## Dispatch statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning | How it is reached |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ringing** | Red | Phones are ringing; nobody has accepted. | The dispatch was raised, or the technician who had accepted released it. |
| **Unanswered** | Amber | Still ringing more than three minutes after the last phone started. | Reached on its own with time. It is a warning, not a change — the phones keep ringing. |
| **On the way** | Blue | A technician accepted and is travelling. | The first technician tapped **Accept dispatch**. |
| **Arrived** | Green | The technician is on site. | They tapped **I've arrived**. |
| **Stood down** | Grey | Cancelled by an operator. | An operator clicked **Stand down**. |
| **Closed** | Grey | Handled and closed by an operator. | An operator clicked **Close dispatch**. |

**Stood down** and **Closed** are final. Nothing reopens a finished dispatch.

## Per-technician ring states

Shown against each name in the **Technicians** section of the detail panel.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent · no ack yet** | The ring was sent but the phone has not confirmed it is playing. |
| **Ringing on phone** | The phone confirmed the siren is playing. |
| **Accepted** | This technician owns the call-out. |
| **Declined** | They declined, with their reason if they gave one. |
| **Stood down** | Their ring was stopped — someone else accepted, or the whole dispatch was stood down. |

## Timers

| Where | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Ringing row, red | Time since the current ring began. Adding technicians restarts it. |
| Accepted row | Time since the technician accepted. |
| Arrived row, green | Total response time — from raising the call-out to arrival on site. Frozen. |

## Fields on the new-dispatch form

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Building** | Yes | Searchable. A dispatch is raised against a building, never a single lift. |
| **People trapped inside?** | Yes | **Yes — entrapment** or **No one trapped**. Entrapment adds a count of people inside. |
| **Caller name** | No | Free text. |
| **Caller phone** | No | Becomes a tap-to-call link in the console and on the technician's phone. |
| **Situation** | No | The caller's own description. The technician reads it before setting off. |
| **Ring technicians** | Yes | At least one. Only active technicians are listed; nothing is pre-selected. |

## Operator actions and when they are available

| Action | Available when |
| --- | --- |
| **＋ New emergency dispatch** | Always, on the Dispatch tab. |
| **Add technician** | While the dispatch is **Ringing** or **Unanswered**. |
| **Stand down** | Any time before the dispatch is stood down or closed. |
| **Close dispatch** | Once a technician has accepted (including after they arrive). |

## Technician actions and when they are available

| Action | Available when |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept dispatch** / **Decline** | While it is ringing and they are one of the people being rung. |
| **Call** | Once accepted, if the operator recorded a caller phone number. |
| **Navigate to building** | Once accepted. Starts the live location share. |
| **I've arrived** | Once accepted, to the technician who accepted. |
| **Can't make it — release this dispatch** | Once accepted, to the technician who accepted. Re-rings everyone else. |

## Who can do what

| | Owner | Admin | Call Centre | Technician |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the Dispatch queue | Yes | Yes | Yes | On their phone only |
| Raise a dispatch | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add technicians, stand down, close | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Be rung by a dispatch | No | No | No | Yes |
| Accept, decline, arrive, release | No | No | No | Yes, if rung |
| Read the on-call rota | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add or delete on-call shifts | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Switch their own on-call state | — | — | — | Yes, in the app |

A **Call Centre** operator is a read-only persona: they see only Portfolio and Dispatch, and cannot create or change anything elsewhere in the dashboard. Assign the role from [Manage your team](/admin/managing-your-team).

<Note>
  Dispatch is optional per workspace. If **Dispatch** is missing from the sidebar it has not been switched on for your team, and the **On call** tab is hidden with it.
</Note>

## Live location and ETA

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **LIVE** with an ETA and a distance | The technician is sharing their position; the estimate is a driving estimate to the building. |
| **No ETA** | No driving estimate could be worked out — most often the building has no map location saved. |
| **updated N min ago** | When their position last moved. |
| **signal quiet** | Their phone has not reported a position for a while. |
| **Not sharing** | The technician is not broadcasting a location. Normal — the share is optional, and it ends when they arrive. |

Live sharing stops automatically when the technician marks themselves arrived or releases the call-out.

## Things a dispatch does not do

* It does not create a [job](/start/glossary), a [record](/admin/records) or an [invoice](/admin/invoices-and-payments). Raise a **Breakdown** job separately if there is billable work — see [Creating a job](/admin/jobs).
* It is not attached to a specific lift, only to a building.
* It does not ring anyone automatically from the rota. An operator always picks the phones.
