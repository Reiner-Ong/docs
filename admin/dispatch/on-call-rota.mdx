The **on-call rota** is the standby schedule: who is expected to answer an emergency at 2am on a Tuesday. It does not change anyone's permissions and it does not ring anyone by itself. Its job is to rank the technician list when an operator [raises a dispatch](/admin/dispatch/raise-a-dispatch), so the right people are the obvious ones to tick.

Open it with **Dispatch** in the left sidebar, then the **On call** tab. It opens on the month view — the view that answers "is next month covered?". The rota also appears on the [Calendar reference](/admin/calendar/reference), because it uses the same calendar shell as the Calendar page, but standby shifts are only ever created and deleted here.

## Who can do what

* **Owners and admins** add and delete shifts.
* **Everyone else** — including call-centre operators and technicians — can read the rota but not change it.
* **Technicians** can also switch themselves on call from their own phone, whatever the rota says. See [Answering a dispatch on your phone](/technician/answering-a-dispatch).

## Add a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click ＋ Add on-call shift">
    The button is at the top right of the rota. You can also click an hour slot in the day or week view, or a day in the month view, to start with that time already filled in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the technician">
    Choose who is on standby. Only active technicians are listed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Starts and Ends">
    Each has its own date and time. A new shift defaults to **18:00 today → 08:00 tomorrow**, the usual overnight cover. A shift may cross midnight, and may run up to **7 days**; anything longer is rejected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose whether it repeats">
    **Repeat** offers *Does not repeat*, *Daily*, *Weekly* or *Monthly*. If you pick one, an **Until** date appears — the rota is built ahead to that date and no further. One entry then covers a standing rota such as "every Monday night".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add shift">
    The shift appears as a red bar on the calendar straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A monthly repeat lands on the same day number each month, and skips any month that does not have that day — a shift on the 31st simply does not occur in February.
</Note>

## Read the rota

Shifts show as red bars labelled with the technician's name, across the **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Agenda** views. Use **‹** and **›** to move a period at a time, **Today** to come back, and the view switch to change how much you see at once.

- **Month** is the coverage view: the gaps are the empty days.
- **Agenda** lists the period's shifts in order. Where nothing is scheduled you will see "No standby cover".
- The **Technicians** list on the left filters the rota. Untick anyone you do not want to see; the count underneath reads "4 of 6 shown". Your choice is remembered in this browser, and a technician who joins later starts out visible.

Click any bar to see the shift's date, time and — if it repeats — its repeat rule.

<Warning>
  LiftAuth does not warn you about gaps. Nothing flags an uncovered night, so check the month before it starts.
</Warning>

## Change or remove a shift

There is no edit — to change a shift, delete it and add the replacement. Shifts cannot be dragged, and one occurrence of a repeating shift cannot be retimed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the shift bar">
    Its details open in a small popover.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Delete">
    A confirmation appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how much to remove">
    A one-off shift offers a single **Delete**. A repeating shift gives you two choices: remove only the occurrence you clicked, which leaves the rest of the series standing, or remove the whole repeating series.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting cannot be undone, and removing the whole series removes **every** occurrence of it, past and future — not just the ones still to come.
</Warning>

## How the rota reaches the dispatcher

When an operator opens the new-dispatch form, LiftAuth works out who is on call at that exact moment and puts them in an **On call** group at the top of the technician list, with the span they are covering — for example _from Mon 18:00 to Tue 08:00_. Everyone else is folded into **Not on call** below. If nobody is rostered for that moment the form says **No one is on call right now**, and the operator rings from the **Not on call** group instead.

The most recent thing that happened wins. If a technician's shift has started they are on call; if they then switch themselves off in their app, they read as off for the rest of that shift; if a later shift begins, they are on again. A technician who switched themselves on outside any shift shows as *self-toggled*.

The grouping is a hint, never a lock — the operator can ring anyone, on call or not.

## What the technician sees

The rota is read-only in the technician app:

- **Profile → ON CALL** shows an **On call** toggle and a link to the **On-call schedule**, with a line telling them either that they are on call now and until when, or when their next shift starts.
- The schedule screen shows the whole team's month, with the technician's own nights marked **You**.
- While they are on call, a blue **On call** banner sits at the bottom of the app.

The toggle is a manual override, for the real world: a technician who is not scheduled can put themselves on call, and one who is scheduled can take themselves off.
