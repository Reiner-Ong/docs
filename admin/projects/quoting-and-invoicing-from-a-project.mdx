---
title: "How to quote and invoice from a project"
description: "Raise the customer quote and the invoice on the project itself, so the project moves with the money."
icon: "receipt"
---

Billing a project works the same way as billing anything else in LiftAuth — the same quote and invoice builders, the same numbering, the same [Billing](/admin/billing/overview) list. What is different is _where_ you start it and _what it does to the project_: a document raised from a stage row belongs to the project, and the project's stages move as that document moves.

## Raise the customer quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Customer quote stage">
    Click the stage row on the project page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Create quote">
    The quote builder opens over the project. The customer and building are already
    filled in from the project and cannot be changed here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the line items and save">
    The saved draft opens straight away, so you can send it without leaving the
    project.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send it, then email it">
    **Send** finalises the quotation and draws its reference number — a draft carries no number yet. It does **not** email anything: the customer only receives the quote when you email it to them, which is a separate step. See [how to quote for work](/admin/billing/quotes) and [how to email a quote or invoice](/admin/billing/emailing-documents).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record their answer">
    Accepted or declined, recorded on the quote as you normally would.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The stage follows the quote by itself:

| The quote is | The Customer quote stage reads |
| --- | --- |
| Draft, or sent and unanswered | In progress |
| **Accepted** (or converted) | **Done** |
| Declined, expired or superseded | In progress — nothing is settled |

<Note>
  **Mark complete** on this stage will not settle a quote the customer has not
  answered. If you try, LiftAuth tells you what it is waiting for: *The customer has
  to accept the quote first — or attach a signed copy*.
</Note>

### If you quote on your own letterhead

Firms that write quotes outside LiftAuth can still use the stage. Attach the signed
copy as a PDF and click **Mark complete** — the hand confirmation settles the stage
exactly as an accepted quote would.

<Warning>
  If you then **requote** — raise a revision, or a second quote after you confirmed
  by hand — the stage drops back to **In progress** and the project's board card
  moves back to Customer quote. That is intentional: a negotiation has reopened. The
  first quote raised after a hand confirmation does not do this.
</Warning>

## Raise the invoice

The **Invoice** stage sits below Work. Once Work is **done**, the band at the top of the project offers **Create invoice**. Skipping Work does not do this — but readiness is only a prompt, so you can raise the invoice from the Invoice row whenever you need to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to bill from">
    On the Invoice row, the button names the quote it will copy from —
    **Create invoice from QUO-…**. Use the caret beside it to pick a different quote
    on the project, or **Start empty**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the lines">
    The line items are copied from the quote you chose. The quote itself is untouched.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the draft, then issue it">
    Every invoice is created as a draft. Issuing it renders the PDF and assigns the invoice number — do that from the document itself. See [how to raise and issue an invoice](/admin/billing/invoices).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email it to the customer">
    Issuing does **not** notify anybody. Until you email the invoice, the customer has not been billed as far as they know. See [how to email a quote or invoice](/admin/billing/emailing-documents).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The stage follows the invoice:

| The invoice is | The Invoice stage reads |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | In progress |
| **Issued**, part paid or paid | **Done** |
| Rejected | Ignored — as if it were not there |

<Note>
  There is no **Mark complete** on the Invoice stage. The stage settles when the invoice is genuinely issued, so the project and your billing records can never disagree. Payment does not hold a project open: an issued invoice is enough to close the project, and the money is tracked in [Billing](/admin/billing/overview).
</Note>

## Work on a document without leaving the project

Click **Open** on any quote or invoice row and it opens over the project. From there
you can do everything you would do on its own page — send, email, edit a draft,
revise, record the customer's decision, convert, download, archive, record a payment,
raise a credit note. When you close it, you are back on the project, and the stage
has already caught up.

<Tip>
  Quotes and invoices raised from a project also appear under **Billing** in the
  sidebar, alongside everything else you have billed. A project is a view onto them,
  not a separate set of books.
</Tip>
