title: "How to create a project"
description: "Create a project for a customer, name who is responsible, and open it at the right stage."

---

A project belongs to one customer — the [Building Manager](/start/glossary), the
company or owner responsible for the building, who you will bill. Everything else,
including the jobs and the documents, is added afterwards.

Before you start, make sure the customer already exists as a
[Building Manager record](/admin/building-managers). You cannot create a project
without choosing one.

## Create the project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Projects">
    Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click New project">
    Click **+ New project** at the top right. Every board column except **Closed**
    also has its own way in — **New project** on the first column, **Start here** on
    the rest. The project is created the same way, and then opens at that column's
    stage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it a title">
    The title is what everyone will read on the board, so make it say what the work
    is: *Door operator replacement — Lift B*. Up to 200 characters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    A short brief, up to 1,000 characters. It is stored with the project but is not
    shown on the project page, so put anything the team needs to read into a stage
    note instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the customer">
    Search for the building manager being billed. This is required, and it cannot be
    changed afterwards — every quote and invoice raised on the project is addressed
    to them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the building (optional)">
    The picker narrows to that customer's buildings. Leave it empty if the work spans
    several of their buildings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Create project">
    LiftAuth gives the project a reference of its own — **PRJ-2026-XXXXXX** — and
    opens it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The customer and the building are fixed at creation. **Edit** on the project page
  changes the title only. If you picked the wrong customer, create the project
  again against the right one.
</Warning>

## Say who is on each stage

Each of the six stages has its own list of people, and it is worth setting them
early: when a stage becomes the project's turn, LiftAuth puts a **Ready** item in
those people's [Inbox](/admin/inbox).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the stage row">
    Click the stage on the project page to expand it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the people">
    Use the assignee picker on the right of the row. Up to 20 people per stage. Only
    owners and admins can be assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a stage has nobody on it, the readiness prompt goes to everyone assigned
  anywhere on the project — and if nobody is assigned at all, to every owner and
  admin in the workspace. Somebody is always told; assignees decide who.
</Tip>

## What happens next

The project opens on **Supplier quote** with all six stages empty. From here:

* [Move it through its stages](/admin/projects/moving-a-project-through-its-stages) — attach documents, link jobs, mark stages complete or skipped.
* [Quote and invoice from it](/admin/projects/quoting-and-invoicing-from-a-project) — so the money side lands back on the project.

The band at the top of the project always names the single next thing to do, with
the button that does it.
