title: "How to move a project through its stages"
description: "Attach documents, link jobs, mark a stage complete, skip one you do not need, and ask a colleague to check your work."

The project page is one column of six stage rows. Settled rows fold down to a
single line; the row the project is on, and anything you have filled in early, stay
open. The band above them names the one next thing to do.

Everything on this page happens on a stage row. What each stage needs before it
counts as done is listed in the [stage reference](/admin/projects/stage-reference).

## Attach a document to a stage

Supplier quote, Customer quote, Procurement, Work and Invoice all take uploaded
documents. **Closed** takes none.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the stage row">
    Click the row to expand it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the file">
    Click the paperclip, or drag the file onto the row — a folded row accepts a drop
    too, and opens when it lands. The row you are over lights up; rows that would
    refuse the file fade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Files must be **PDF, PNG or JPEG**, up to **10 MB** each. If you drop a batch, the
good files still upload and LiftAuth names the ones it refused. An upload in
progress can be cancelled.

Click a document to read it in the preview drawer. To take one off again, remove it
from the row — but only while the stage is unlocked.

<Note>
  Attaching is not completing. On **Supplier quote**, **Customer quote** and
  **Procurement**, a document moves the stage to **In progress** and no further.
  Somebody still has to say it is done.
</Note>

## Mark a stage complete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the row has what it needs">
    **Mark complete** stays greyed out until it does. The information icon beside it
    says exactly what is missing — *Attach a document first*, *Every linked job has
    to be signed first*, and so on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Mark complete">
    The stage turns **Done** and shows a padlock. Your name and the date go on the row
    and into the activity log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A completed stage is **locked**: no attaching, no removing, no re-completing. Click
**Mark incomplete** to unlock it.

<Warning>
  **Invoice** and **Closed** have no Mark complete button, and that is deliberate.
  The Invoice stage settles when the invoice is actually issued, and Closed settles
  when somebody closes the project. See
  [quoting and invoicing](/admin/projects/quoting-and-invoicing-from-a-project).
</Warning>

## Record the work

The **Work** stage holds two kinds of thing, and you can mix them:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your own jobs" icon="wrench">
    Click **\+ Schedule job** to raise a new [job](/admin/jobs/overview) and link it in one go, or **Link existing job** to attach one you already have. Each linked job shows its status and, once signed, its record.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A subcontractor's work" icon="file-lines">
    Attach the supplier's service record to the stage as a PDF or photo.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Link existing job** searches your jobs by building, lift, technician or reference.
It offers jobs at the project's building first, then the customer's other
buildings, then everything — a job belonging to a different customer is badged as
such, so you can see what you are about to link.

How Work settles:

* Every linked job must be **Signed** or **Closed**.
* If nothing is attached to the stage, it turns **Done** on its own the moment the
  last job is signed.
* If there is an attached record as well, click **Mark complete** once the jobs are
  signed.

<Warning>
  A cancelled job never counts as finished and will hold the stage open forever.
  Detach it from the Work row instead.
</Warning>

## Skip a stage you do not need

Not every project has a supplier or a purchase order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Skip on the stage row">
    **Skip** only appears on a stage that is empty and unlocked.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give a reason (optional)">
    The reason is shown on the row afterwards, so the next person knows why the stage
    was passed over.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A skipped stage reads **Skipped** and counts as settled — the project can close over
it. Click **Unskip** to bring it back. A skipped stage stays skipped even if a
document later lands on it.

**Closed** cannot be skipped. **Invoice** can — skip it for a warranty callback or a
goodwill repair you are not billing for.

## Ask a colleague to review a stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a reviewer on the stage row">
    Use the review picker. You can ask several people at once; you cannot ask yourself.
  </Step>

  <Step title="They answer on the same row">
    The reviewer sees the request on the stage and clicks **Approve**, or
    **Request changes** with a comment explaining what is wrong.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The stage badge shows *Review requested*, *Changes requested* or *Approved*, and the
same items appear in the reviewer's [Inbox](/admin/inbox).

<Note>
  Reviews advise, they do not gate. Requesting changes does not unlock a completed
  stage and does not stop the project moving. A stage with changes requested stays
  open on the page until it is dealt with, so it cannot be folded away and forgotten.
</Note>

## Leave a note

Click **+ Add note** on a stage row for anything the team needs to read — a phone
call with the supplier, why the price changed. Notes sit on the stage they were
written on and appear in the activity log.

## Watch what has happened

The pane on the right of the project has two tabs:

* **Activity** — every event on the project, newest first: documents attached, stages
  completed and skipped, jobs linked, reviews, reopens.
* **Chat** — the project's own conversation thread. You can drag a stage document
  straight into it to share it with the team.

Drag the divider to resize the pane, or collapse it entirely; LiftAuth remembers
your choice.
