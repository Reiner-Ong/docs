---
title: "How to close a project and send the paperwork"
description: "Download the project's documents, close it when every stage is settled, email the customer one package, and reopen it if you have to."
icon: "box"
---

Closing is the project's one manual final act. It freezes the project: no more
attaching, completing, skipping or assigning until somebody reopens it.

## Download the documents at any time

You do not have to close a project to get its paperwork.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Download package">
    It is at the top right of the project page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the zip">
    Documents are foldered by stage in pipeline order — `01-supplier-quote`, `02-customer-quote`, `03-internal-procurement`, `04-work`, `05-invoice`. The zip holds everything: uploaded files, quote and invoice PDFs, and the [records](/start/glossary) of every linked job.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The download includes the internal stages, because it stays inside your office.
  What you **email** to the customer does not — see below.
</Note>

## Close the project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Settle every stage first">
    Close is only allowed once all five stages before Closed are **Done** or
    **Skipped**. Nothing may be left empty or in progress.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Close project">
    The dialog lists all six stages with a verdict on each. Anything still open is
    named, with **Go to stage** to deal with it and **Skip** if it was never needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    The project is stamped closed, with your name and the date, and moves to the
    **Closed** column.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The information icon beside a disabled **Close project** button tells you what is
  in the way — *Work is still open*, or *3 stages still open*. The dialog behind the
  button is the only place that names them all, so open it even while it is blocked.
</Tip>

## Email the package to the customer

**Email package** only becomes available once the project is closed. Until then a
package would be a snapshot of work still in flight, sitting in the customer's inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Email package">
    The customer's contact email is filled in for you. Add or remove addresses as you
    need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the format">
    **One PDF** merges everything into a single document with a contents page.
    **Zip** keeps the files separate, foldered by stage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose which stages to include">
    Each stage is a checkbox with its document count. **Supplier quote** and
    **Procurement** are internal and are never offered — nothing on them can be
    emailed to the building manager.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a message and send">
    LiftAuth confirms who it went to. If a file could not be merged into the single
    PDF, it is named and sent as a separate attachment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Sending is recorded on the project only after every address has actually been sent
  to, so the activity log never claims a send that failed.
</Warning>

## Reopen a closed project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Reopen project">
    On a closed project this is the only filled button on the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give a reason">
    A reason is required, up to 200 characters. Every reopen is kept, so a project
    reopened twice keeps both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A reopened project goes back to the stage its documents imply, **not** the stage it
was on when it was closed — if a stage no longer holds what settled it, that is where the project lands. The dialog names where it will land before you confirm. A project that is
still fully invoiced comes back ready to close again.
