# Add a Lift

A <Tooltip tip="One physical elevator unit at a building." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Lift</Tooltip> must be registered with [BCA LEAP](https://www2.bca.gov.sg/leap/PTOEnquiry/Search), Singapore's government lift-register service, before you add it to LiftAuth. LiftAuth uses the lift's BCA PTO Enquiry URL—the link to its BCA equipment record—to look up official equipment details.

## Before you start

You need either:

- the URL of the lift's BCA PTO Enquiry page; or
- a clear photo of the QR code on the lift's PTO sticker.

You also need a <Tooltip tip="One property or site where lifts are installed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Building</Tooltip> in LiftAuth. The building owns the address and the assigned <Tooltip tip="The company or owner responsible for a building. They receive invoices and sign completed work." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Building Manager</Tooltip> for the lift.

## Add the lift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration form">
    From **Lifts** in the left sidebar, select **Add lift**. If you are already viewing a building in **Portfolio**, select **Add lift** from that building instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look up the BCA record">
    Paste the BCA PTO Enquiry URL, scan the QR code, or upload a clear photo of the QR code. LiftAuth looks up the registered equipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Check the BCA equipment ID and expiry information, then select the lift type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the building">
    Select the building where the lift is installed. If you started from a building in Portfolio, it is already selected. If you started from the Lifts page and the building is missing, select **Add building** to create it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the registration">
    Add the manufacturer and any other details you have, then select **Register lift**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  LiftAuth prevents duplicate registrations when the lift is already registered in your company.
</Warning>

## What happens next

The lift now has its own jobs and <Tooltip tip="A fault or problem reported against one lift and tracked until it is closed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">issues</Tooltip>. A <Tooltip tip="A building-level service schedule that covers one or more lifts." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Maintenance Plan</Tooltip> can cover the lift with other lifts at the same building.

For the wider relationship between buildings, Building Managers, lifts, and issues, see [manage your portfolio](/admin/portfolio).
