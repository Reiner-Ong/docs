title: "Raising an Issue"

---

An <Tooltip tip="A fault or problem reported against one lift and tracked until it is closed." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">issue</Tooltip> records one problem against one lift. It stays **Open** until your team resolves or closes it.

<Note>
  Owners, Admins, and Technicians can raise, edit, and close issues. Call Centre users can view issues but cannot change them.
</Note>

## Raise an issue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Issues tab">
    In the left sidebar, select <Tooltip tip="The dashboard area where your LiftAuth company keeps its buildings, Building Manager records, lifts, and issues together." cta="Read the glossary" href="/start/glossary">Portfolio</Tooltip>. Then select **Issues**.

    <Frame>
      ![The Portfolio Issues tab showing status and severity filters, the issues list, and the Raise Issue button.](/images/admin-issues-list.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Raise Issue">
    Select **Raise Issue** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the lift and severity">
    Select the lift with the problem. Then choose how urgently it needs attention:

    - **Critical** — the lift is unsafe or cannot be used.
    - **Major** — the problem affects normal use and may take the lift out of service.
    - **Minor** — the problem has little immediate impact and can wait for a planned visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Enter a short, specific **Title**. Add a **Description** that tells the next person what happened, where it happened, and anything already tried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add photos or videos">
    Add clear photos or videos when they will help someone understand the problem. This is optional.

    <Frame>
      ![The Raise Issue window with fields for lift, severity, title, description, photos, and videos.](/images/admin-raise-issue-dialog.png)
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      You can add up to 10 files. Photos can be JPEG, PNG, or WEBP files up to 10 MB each. Videos can be MP4 or MOV files up to 64 MB each, with no more than 5 videos.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise the issue">
    Select **Raise Issue**. The issue appears in the **Open** list with its own reference, such as **ISS-000123**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are already viewing a lift, open its **Issues** tab and select **Raise Issue**. LiftAuth fills in the lift for you.
</Tip>

## Find an issue

Open **Portfolio**, then select **Issues**. The page shows open issues first.

Use the controls above the list to:

- show **All**, **Open**, or **Closed** issues;
- filter by **Critical**, **Major**, or **Minor** severity; and
- sort by the date the issue was raised.

Select any issue in the list to open its details.

## Update an open issue

1. Open the issue from the **Issues** list.
2. Select **Edit Issue**.
3. Change the title, description, severity, photos, or videos.
4. Select **Save Changes**.

<Frame>
  ![An open issue showing its severity, lift, raised date, description, and available actions.](/images/admin-issue-detail.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  You cannot edit an issue after it is closed.
</Warning>

<Note>
  You may also see **Raise Quote** on an open issue. Use it when the fault needs a repair quote. Owners and Admins may see **Classify fault** when fault classification is enabled for the company.
</Note>

## Close an issue

Close an issue when no more work needs to be tracked against it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the issue">
    Find the issue in **Portfolio** → **Issues**, then select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Close Issue">
    Select **Close Issue** on the right side of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a reason">
    Choose the reason that best describes what happened:

    - **Resolved** — the problem was fixed.
    - **Won't Fix** — the problem is known, but no work will be done.
    - **Duplicate** — the same problem is already recorded as another issue.
    - **Decommissioned** — the lift was taken out of service.

    <Frame>
      ![The Close Issue window with a reason field and Close Issue button.](/images/admin-close-issue-dialog.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Close Issue**. The issue moves to the **Closed** list and can no longer be edited.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a technician works on the issue

During a job on the lift, a technician can see its open issues and link the ones they are working on.

- If the technician submits a linked issue as **Resolved**, LiftAuth closes it automatically.
- If the technician submits it as **Not Resolved**, the issue stays open.
