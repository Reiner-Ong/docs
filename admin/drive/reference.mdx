Everything Drive shows, in one place. For the tasks themselves, start at the
[Drive overview](/admin/drive/overview).

## Folder types

| Folder | One per | Holds | Subtitle on the folder card |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Building** | Building | Service records | Number of lifts, or number of records for a building you own no lift in |
| **Unassigned** | — (one only) | Records for lifts not attached to any building | Number of lifts |
| **Customer** | Building manager | Quotes, invoices, payments, credit notes | `Quotes · Invoices · Payments` |

Building folders appear on their own, for every building your firm has a lift in
and every building your firm holds a record for. Customer folders appear for every
building manager on your account.

The customer folder's subtitle names three document kinds, but the folder itself opens on **four** tabs — Quotes, Invoices, Payments and Credit notes. Credit notes are not in the subtitle; they are still in the folder.

<h2 id="what-ends-up-in-drive">
  What ends up in Drive
</h2>

There is no upload button in Drive. Documents arrive in one of three ways.

| Document | How it gets there |
| --- | --- |
| Service record | Created automatically when a technician submits a completed form on a [job](/admin/jobs/overview) — a job carrying several forms files several records |
| Past visit added by hand | Added with **Record past visits** on a building page or a maintenance contract page. This is the one manual path into Drive. It creates a **record only — no job** — and the record is metadata plus an optional legacy PDF: date, lifts, technician and notes, with no item results and no signature |
| Quote, invoice, payment, credit note | Created on the [Billing](/admin/billing/overview) pages, then filed under the customer |

A lift with no building is grouped in Drive under a folder named **Unassigned**, listed after the real building folders. Its records are in there, not missing.

## Building folder columns

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Checkbox | Ticks the row for **Download selected** |
| **Name** | The record's reference |
| **Lift** | The equipment ID of every lift the visit covered, or `—` |
| **Kind** | Appears only if a building folder ever holds more than one kind of file. Building folders hold service records, so in practice this column stays hidden |
| **Sign-off** | Whether the record is still waiting on a signature — see the badges below |
| **Date** | The date the visit was carried out. Click the heading to reverse the order |
| (last column) | Download arrow, or `— no document` |

### Sign-off badges

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending signature** (amber) | Nobody has signed yet |
| **Signed** (green) | The record has left the unsigned state — **either** the building manager signed it, **or** the technician skipped signing. The badge does not tell the two apart |

### Status in the preview window

The panel beside the PDF shows a fuller status than the list does.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Unsigned** | Nobody has signed yet. The sign-off options are offered, and the document on screen is a live preview generated on demand |
| **Signed** | Signed by the building manager, or closed out by the technician skipping signing. A customer-signed record has its stored PDF from the moment of signing; a skipped one has no stored PDF until the job is closed |
| **Complete** | Signed, with the stored PDF in place |

A skipped record and a genuinely customer-signed one are indistinguishable here: both read **Signed** and then **Complete**. The generated PDF is the only place the difference shows — it drops the building-manager signature row when the signature was skipped.

**Download PDF has no status gate at all.** It is offered whenever the document has loaded, so an **Unsigned** record downloads just as readily as a **Signed** or **Complete** one; for an unsigned record what you get is the live preview of the document the building manager will see. What closing the job guarantees is that _every_ record ends up with a stored PDF.

<h2 id="when-a-record-has-no-pdf">
  When a record has no PDF
</h2>

Two situations leave a row with nothing to open. Neither can be repaired from
Drive, and both are skipped when you download a folder.

| What you see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `— no document` in place of the download arrow, and the row cannot be opened or ticked | A past visit typed in by hand with no report attached |
| **Signature image unreadable** or **Photo unreadable** when you open the record | The PDF could not be produced from what was captured on site |

## Actions

| Action | Where it is |
| --- | --- |
| Search buildings and customers | Search box at the top of Drive |
| Search records | Search box inside a building folder, matching reference and lift |
| Sort by date | The **Date** column heading |
| Preview a record | Click the row |
| Download one record | Download arrow on the row |
| **Download selected (n)** | Appears once a row is ticked; covers the current page only |
| **Download all** | Top right of the folder; the whole folder as one zip |
| **Send to building manager** | Preview window, unsigned records only — hidden once a record is signed or the signature was skipped |
| **Generate new signing link** | Preview window, unsigned records only — hidden once a record is signed or the signature was skipped |
| Download a quote, invoice or credit note | PDF icon on the row in a customer folder |

## Who can see what

Drive only ever shows your own firm's documents. Roles are [Owner, Admin, Technician and Call Center](/start/glossary#who-does-what) — **Call Center** is the role's name on screen.

|  | Owner | Admin | Technician | Call Center |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Drive** in the sidebar | Yes | Yes | Yes | No — the nav item is hidden |
| Open a building folder and list its records | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes, by typing the URL |
| Download a record's PDF | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Generate a record signing link | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Credit notes | Yes | Yes | No | No |

A technician has a working Drive: they sign in, **Drive** is in their sidebar, and they can open a building folder, read its records, download a record's PDF and generate a signing link.

Inside a customer folder, following a row through to the quote's or invoice's own page needs access to the billing pages. A technician who tries is redirected to their **Jobs** page rather than shown an error.

<Note>
  **A record cannot be corrected.** It cannot be edited once it exists, by anyone — there is no amend, no resubmit and no edit screen in either app. A signed record cannot even be deleted; an unsigned one can be deleted by an owner or admin, but never amended, and nothing can re-create it afterwards. Drive is a place to read and download. If something is wrong on a record, raise it as an [issue](/admin/issues) or contact support.
</Note>
