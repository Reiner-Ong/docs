A record is only finished once the building manager has signed it. If the
technician could not get a signature on site, you can send the request straight
from Drive.

Records still waiting are easy to spot: the **Sign-off** column shows an amber
**Pending signature** badge.

## Open the record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the building folder">
    Click **Drive** in the sidebar, then the building.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the record's row">
    The preview window opens with the PDF on the left and the sign-off panel on
    the right. If the record is unsigned, the panel offers the two options below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose how to send it

There are three routes to a signature. Two of them are here in the sign-off panel; the third is the technician collecting it on site in the mobile app, which happens before the record ever reaches this page.

**Send to building manager**

LiftAuth emails the sign-off link to every member the building manager's own firm has assigned to that building — they are assigned by that firm, not by you. Click the **Send to building manager** card in the panel once; nothing else is needed.

<Warning>
  If that firm has assigned nobody to the building, the send still reports success but no email goes anywhere — the request only appears in the firm's own inbox. If a customer says they never received it, ask them who is assigned to the building on their side, or share a signing link yourself instead.
</Warning>

**Generate a signing link**

LiftAuth creates a link you can share yourself, by WhatsApp, SMS or any other means. On the **Generate a signing link** card, click **Generate new signing link**, then **Copy link**.

Either way, the link itself is a single-use token, so whoever opens it can sign without a LiftAuth account. An account is only needed to _receive_ the email — which is why a link you share yourself reaches anybody, while the email route depends on someone being assigned at the customer's firm.

Once they sign, the record's status changes to **Signed** on its own and the job turns up in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox) ready to close.

## What to know about signing links

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A link is valid for 7 days" icon="clock">
    After that it stops working and you generate a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Generating again does not cancel the old link" icon="link-slash">
    Each time you generate or send, you create an **additional** link. Every link
    already out there stays valid until it is used or expires.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A link can only be used once" icon="check">
    As soon as someone signs with it, the link is spent. The record is signed and
    cannot be changed afterwards.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Sending the email request is an owner or admin action. Technicians can still generate and copy a signing link, and can get a signature on site from the mobile app — see [How to get the job signed off](/technician/signing-off-a-job).
</Note>

<Warning>
  A past visit you typed in by hand cannot be signed — there was no technician
  on site and no signature to collect. The sign-off options do not appear on
  those records.
</Warning>
