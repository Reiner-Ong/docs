Most records are finished by the building manager signing them. If the technician could not get a signature on site, you can send the request straight from Drive.

Records still waiting are easy to spot: the **Sign-off** column shows an amber **Pending signature** badge.

<Warning>
  This page is for records that are still unsigned. A record where the technician **skipped signing** has already left the unsigned state and can never be sent for signature again — see [If the technician skipped signing](#if-the-technician-skipped-signing) below.
</Warning>

## Open the record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the building folder">
    Click **Drive** in the sidebar, then the building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the record's row">
    The preview window opens with the PDF on the left and the sign-off panel on the right. If the record is unsigned, the panel offers the two options below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose how to send it

There are three routes to a signature. Two of them are here in the sign-off panel; the third is the technician collecting it on site in the mobile app, which happens before the record ever reaches this page.

**Send to building manager**

LiftAuth emails the sign-off link to every member the building manager's own firm has assigned to that building — they are assigned by that firm, not by you. Click the **Send to building manager** card in the panel once; nothing else is needed.

<Note>
  Because the recipients are whoever that firm has assigned to the building, who gets the email is not something you control. If a customer says they never received it, ask them who is assigned to the building on their side, or share a signing link yourself instead — a link you send reaches anybody.
</Note>

**Generate a signing link**

LiftAuth creates a link you can share yourself, by WhatsApp, SMS or any other means. On the **Generate a signing link** card, click **Generate new signing link**, then **Copy link**.

Either way, the link itself is a single-use token, so whoever opens it can sign without a LiftAuth account. An account is only needed to _receive_ the email — which is why a link you share yourself reaches anybody, while the email route depends on someone being assigned at the customer's firm.

Once they sign, the record's status changes to **Signed** on its own and the job turns up in your [Inbox](/admin/inbox) ready to close.

## What to know about signing links

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A link is valid for 7 days" icon="clock">
    After that it stops working and you generate a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Generating again does not cancel the old link" icon="link-slash">
    Each time you generate or send, you create an **additional** link. Every link already out there stays valid until it is used or expires.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A link can only be used once" icon="check">
    As soon as someone signs with it, the link is spent. The signature is the last thing that can be written to a record: a record cannot be edited once it exists, by anyone, and a signed one cannot even be deleted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If the technician skipped signing {#if-the-technician-skipped-signing}

A technician who cannot get a signature on site can **skip signing**, which ends the record without a building-manager signature. Three things follow, and the last is the one to plan around.

- **You will not chase it by mistake.** A skipped record leaves the unsigned state, so it never sits in your list wearing **Pending signature** as though a customer still owed you a signature.
- **But you cannot tell it apart from a record the customer really signed.** In the dashboard both read **Signed** and then **Complete**, in exactly the same way; no screen distinguishes them. The PDF is the honest record — it drops the building-manager signature row altogether — so open the document when you need to know which of the two happened.
- **Skipping is final.** A skipped record can never be sent for signature again, from Drive or anywhere else. **Send to building manager** and **Generate new signing link** are both hidden on it, and any signing links still outstanding for that record stop working the moment the technician skips.

<Note>
  Technicians get Drive too. A technician can open a building folder, read the records in it, download a record's PDF and generate a signing link, and can collect a signature on site in the mobile app — see [How to get the job signed off](/technician/signing-off-a-job).
</Note>

<Warning>
  A **past visit** added by hand has no signature to collect: no technician was on site and no form was filled in. It is metadata plus an optional legacy PDF, so sign-off does not apply to it.
</Warning>
