---
title: "How to download records from Drive"
description: "Save one record, a selection of records, or an entire building folder as a zip."
icon: "download"
---

Every record in a building folder is a PDF you can save. Drive gives you three
ways to do it, depending on how many you need.

## Download one record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the building folder">
    Click **Drive** in the sidebar, then the building you want.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the download arrow">
    The download arrow sits at the right-hand end of the record's row. The file is
    saved under the record's own reference, so it stays identifiable once it is on
    your computer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also open a record first — click the row — read the PDF in the preview
  window, and download it from there once you are sure it is the right one.
</Tip>

## Download several records at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the records you want">
    Each row has a checkbox on the left. The checkbox in the header row ticks and
    unticks everything on the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Download selected">
    A **Download selected (n)** button appears next to **Download all** as soon as
    one row is ticked. LiftAuth collects the PDFs into a single zip file named
    after the building.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your selection only covers the page you are looking at, and it is cleared when
  you turn the page or change the sort order. To take a whole folder in one go,
  use **Download all** below rather than ticking page by page.
</Warning>

## Download a whole building folder

Click **Download all**, at the top right of the folder. This takes **every record
in the building**, not only the page on screen and not only the rows matching your
search, and saves them as one zip file named after the building.

Large folders take a moment: the PDFs are collected one at a time, and a
**Preparing… 3/40** counter next to the button shows how far along it is. Stay on
the page until the download starts.

<Note>
  Two kinds of row are left out of a zip, because there is no document to put in
  it: a past visit typed in by hand with no report attached, and a record whose
  PDF could not be produced. The rest of the folder still downloads normally. See
  [When a record has no PDF](/admin/drive/reference#when-a-record-has-no-pdf).
</Note>

## Downloading a customer's quotes and invoices

Account documents are not part of a building folder — they live in the customer's
own folder, and are downloaded one at a time. See
[How to find a customer's quotes, invoices and payments](/admin/drive/customer-documents).
