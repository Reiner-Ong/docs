A customer folder collects everything commercial for one
[building manager](/admin/building-managers) — what you quoted them, what you
billed them, what they paid, and anything you credited back. It is the folder to
open when someone asks "what have we sent this customer?"

<Note>
  Customer folders are read-only. You can read and download here; creating, editing, approving and sending all happen on the [Billing](/admin/billing/overview) pages.
</Note>

## Open the folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Drive">
    Click **Drive** in the left sidebar, under **Operations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scroll to Customers">
    The **Customers** section sits below **Buildings**. Every building manager on
    your account has a folder, even if it is still empty. The search box at the
    top of Drive filters customers by name too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    The folder opens on **Quotes**. **Invoices**, **Payments** and **Credit notes**
    are the other three tabs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each tab holds

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Quotes" icon="file-lines">
    Every quote raised for this customer, with its status, the date it is valid
    until, and its total. A quote that has been revised carries an **R1**, **R2**
    marker next to its number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invoices" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    The invoices that have actually been issued — with their status, invoice date,
    due date and total. An invoice past its due date with money still outstanding
    is flagged **Overdue** in red.

    [Draft invoices](/start/glossary) are deliberately left out: until an admin approves one it is not a document the customer has seen. Review drafts on the [Billing](/admin/billing/overview) page instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payments" icon="money-bill">
    Every payment recorded against this customer, with the amount, the date it was
    paid, and how it came in — Manual, Bank, Stripe or PayNow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credit notes" icon="receipt">
    Amounts credited back to the customer, each showing whether it is still open,
    has been applied, or was voided, and whether it applies to one invoice or to
    the customer's account as a whole.

    This tab is visible to owners and admins only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Download a document

Click the PDF icon at the right-hand end of a row. Quotes, invoices and credit
notes each download as a single PDF. Payments have no document of their own —
they are entries against an invoice.

## Open the full document

Click anywhere else on a row to leave Drive and open that quote, invoice or
payment on its own page, where you can act on it.

<Note>
  Opening the full page needs access to the billing pages. Technicians can read
  the lists inside a customer folder, but following a row through will send them
  back to their Jobs page.
</Note>
