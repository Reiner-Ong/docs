Stock levels in LiftAuth are never typed in directly. You record what happened — a delivery arrived, units were taken out, stock moved to a van — and LiftAuth adjusts the counts. Each of those entries is a **movement**, and the full list of them is the part's history.

There are three kinds:

| Movement | What it means | Effect on the count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deposit** | Stock came in — a supplier delivery, a return, a stocktake correction upwards | Adds to one warehouse |
| **Withdraw** | Stock went out — used on a job, scrapped, a correction downwards | Subtracts from one warehouse |
| **Transfer** | Stock moved from one of your warehouses to another | Subtracts from one, adds to the other. Your total stays the same |

## Record a movement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the part">
    **Inventory → Parts**, then click the part.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Move stock">
    The **Move stock** dialog opens.

    <Tip>
      **\+ Move stock** in the part's **Stock by location** section opens the same dialog, if that is the section you are already looking at.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the Type">
    **Deposit (add stock)**, **Withdraw (remove stock)**, or **Transfer (move between warehouses)**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the warehouse">
    For a deposit or a withdrawal, pick the one **Warehouse** involved. For a transfer, pick **From** and **To** — LiftAuth will not let you pick the same one for both.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the quantity">
    A whole number, at least 1. It is always a positive number — the type you chose decides whether it is added or subtracted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a note (optional but worth it)">
    A short line saying why, for example *received PO-2231* or *used on REP-2026-4F1A*. The note is the only place that reason is kept.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Record">
    The counts update immediately, and the entry appears at the top of **Recent movements**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Get into the habit of writing a note every time. Six months later, a bare `−3` tells you nothing.
</Tip>

<h2 id="transfer-move-stock-between-warehouses">
  Transfers between warehouses
</h2>

Use a transfer, not a withdrawal followed by a deposit. A transfer is one entry, it can never leave the two counts out of step, and the history shows it in one line as *Tuas Central Store → Van 2*.

## Reading the movement history

Open a part and scroll to **Recent movements**. Each row shows:

* **Change** — `+4` in green for a deposit, `−4` in red for a withdrawal, or a plain number for a transfer, which does not change your total.
* **Type** — Deposit, Withdraw, or Transfer.
* **Location** — the warehouse, or `From → To` for a transfer.
* **Note** — what you typed, or `—`.
* **Date** — when it was recorded.

Longer histories are paged; use the page controls beneath the table.

<Note>
  Movements cannot be edited or deleted — the history is a permanent record. If you recorded the wrong figure, record a correcting movement in the other direction and say so in the note.
</Note>

## When LiftAuth refuses a withdrawal

You cannot take out more than a warehouse holds. If you try, the dialog shows an error saying there is insufficient stock and nothing is recorded. The same applies to the *From* side of a transfer.

If the app says there is nothing on the shelf but there plainly is, the shelf is right and LiftAuth is behind — record a **Deposit** for the units that were never entered, note it as a stocktake correction, and then do the withdrawal.

## Doing it from the phone

Technicians record movements the same way in the mobile app: **Profile → Tools → Inventory**, open the part, tap **Move stock**. Inventory needs a connection — it does not work offline.
