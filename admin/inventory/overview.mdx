Inventory is where you keep the list of spare parts your company works with, and the count of how many of each one you actually have in each storage location.

Open it by clicking **Inventory** in the left sidebar. The page has two tabs — **Parts** and **Warehouses**.

## The three things Inventory keeps track of

These three are easy to mix up, so it is worth separating them before you start.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The parts catalogue" icon="list">
    The list of *kinds* of part you deal with — "Door Operator Motor", "Landing Interlock Contact". One entry per kind of part, with a photo, a category, a brand, and prices. It does not say how many you have.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Warehouses" icon="warehouse">
    The places you store parts — a central store, a depot, or a service van. A warehouse does not have to have a fixed address.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stock on hand" icon="hashtag">
    The number of units of one part sitting in one warehouse. LiftAuth works this number out for you from the stock movements you record — you never type the number in directly after the first time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

So a part called *Door Operator Motor* might have **4 on hand** in total: 3 in the Tuas Central Store and 1 in Van 2. Those two numbers are its stock levels, and every change to them is written down as a **movement**.

<Note>
  Inventory is an optional feature. If you see "Inventory isn't enabled for this workspace", contact your account team to turn it on.
</Note>

## Getting started, in order

If Inventory is new to your company, do it in this order — a part cannot hold stock until there is a warehouse to hold it in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your warehouses first">
    **Inventory → Warehouses → + Add Warehouse**. Even one, called something like "Main Store", is enough to begin. See [Setting up warehouses](/admin/inventory/setting-up-warehouses).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your parts">
    **Inventory → Parts → + Add Part**. You can set each part's opening stock while you create it. See [Adding a part](/admin/inventory/adding-a-part).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep the counts honest from then on">
    Every time parts arrive, get used, or get shifted, record a movement. See [Moving stock](/admin/inventory/moving-stock).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Inventory connects to the rest of LiftAuth

* **Quotes and invoices.** A part's **unit price** is its catalogue list price. When you add a _Part_ line to a quote or an invoice, LiftAuth fills in the description and the price from the catalogue, so you are not retyping them. See [Billing](/admin/billing/overview).
* **Jobs.** When an accepted quote that contains part lines is converted into a repair <Tooltip tip="One visit's worth of work at one building, which can cover several lifts." cta="Glossary" href="/start/glossary">job</Tooltip>, the job carries a **Parts required** list. That list is a reminder, not a deduction — see [Using parts on a job](/admin/inventory/using-parts-on-a-job).
* **The technician mobile app.** Technicians reach the same catalogue from **Profile → Tools → Inventory** on their phone, where they can search parts, add a part, and record a stock movement.
