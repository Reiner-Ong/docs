Everything on this page starts at **Inventory → Parts** in the left sidebar.

## Search by name or part number

Type into the **Search parts…** box. The list narrows as you type.

The search matches two things:

* the part's **name** — for example `door operator`
* the part's **Part No.** — LiftAuth's own reference, for example `PRT-2026-A1B2C3`

It does **not** match the manufacturer's part code. If you only have a code printed on the component, use the family and brand filters below to narrow the list, then check the photos.

## Narrow the list with filters

Two dropdowns sit beside the search box:

* **Family** — the category of component, such as Doors or Safety gear.
* **Brand** — the manufacturer, or Generic.

Pick a value and the pill turns solid; click the **✕** on the pill to clear that one filter, or click **Clear all** to clear both. The count on the right — for example *42 parts* — always shows how many parts match what you have selected.

<Tip>
  Search and filters work together. Searching `motor` with **Family: Doors** selected finds only door motors.
</Tip>

## Switch between list and grid

Two small buttons near **+ Add Part** change how results are shown:

* **List view** — a table with Part, Part No., Family, Brand, and On Hand. Best for scanning quantities.
* **Grid view** — photo cards with the quantity in the corner. Best when you are trying to recognise a component by sight.

LiftAuth remembers whichever view you last used.

## See archived parts

Parts you have archived are hidden by default. Click **Show archived** to include them — they appear with an **Archived** badge. Click **Hide archived** to leave them out again.

## Open a part to see where its stock is

Click any row or card to open the part. The page shows:

* **Overview** — the photos, family, brand, Part No., part code, and total on hand. Click the main photo to enlarge it.
* **Stock by location** — a row per warehouse, with the quantity held there and when it last changed.
* **Recent movements** — the history of every deposit, withdrawal, and transfer for this part.

To go the other way — "what is in the Tuas store?" — open **Inventory → Warehouses** and click the warehouse. See [Setting up warehouses](/admin/inventory/setting-up-warehouses).

## Looking up a part you do not stock yet

The pages above search **your** catalogue. LiftAuth also has a separate **Part Search** page, at the address `/part-search` in your dashboard, which searches a catalogue of lift components collected from major suppliers — useful when you are trying to identify or source something you have never bought before. It shows product names, part numbers, key specs, and links out to the supplier's page or brochure.

<Note>
  Part Search is in beta and is not linked from the sidebar yet. It is a lookup tool only — nothing found there is added to your own catalogue or your stock. To stock it, [add it as a part](/admin/inventory/adding-a-part).
</Note>

## On the phone

A technician can search the same catalogue from the mobile app: **Profile → Tools → Inventory**. It has the same search box and Category and Brand filters, plus **Identify a part** — take or upload a photo of a component and the app suggests the closest matches in your catalogue.
