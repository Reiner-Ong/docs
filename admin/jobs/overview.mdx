Jobs are how LiftAuth tracks service visits. One job is one visit, to one
building, by one technician or one team.

## The shape of it

A job carries a building, its lifts, the forms the technician fills in, and one
assignee. It moves through six statuses, and each status belongs to somebody:

**Open → Scheduled → Work Done → Signed → Closed**, with **Cancelled** as the way
out at any point before Closed.

You schedule it. The technician moves it to **Work Done**. The building manager's
signature moves it to **Signed**. You close it. Nobody skips a step, and nothing
moves backwards.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check you have what a job needs">
    A building with lifts and a building manager, at least one record template, and
    an active technician. Missing any of these and the scheduling dialog will stop
    you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule the visit">
    **Jobs** in the sidebar, then **Schedule Job**. Pick the building and the
    records, then the person and the time.
    [Full steps →](/admin/jobs/how-to-schedule-a-job)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch it come back">
    The board's **Work Done** and **Signed** columns fill up as technicians finish.
    Your Inbox lists everything waiting on you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close it">
    Review the record and click **Close job**.
    [Full steps →](/admin/jobs/how-to-close-a-job)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If the same building needs servicing on a regular cycle, do not schedule each visit by hand. Set up a [Maintenance Contract](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview) and LiftAuth creates and assigns the jobs for you.
</Tip>
