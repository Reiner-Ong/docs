Jobs are how LiftAuth tracks service visits. One job is one visit, to one
building, by one technician or one team. The building manager signs it off; some screens call them the customer, and it is the same party.

## The shape of it

A job carries a building, its lifts, the forms the technician fills in, and one
assignee. It moves through six statuses, and each status belongs to somebody:

**Open → Scheduled → Work Done → Signed → Closed**, with **Cancelled** as the way
out at any point before Closed.

You schedule it. The technician moves it to **Work Done**. The building manager's signature moves it to **Signed**. You close it. Nothing ever moves backwards — but a job can skip **Open** entirely: the **Schedule Job** button on the Jobs page creates a job that is already **Scheduled**.

## Getting started

A job needs a building with lifts and a building manager, at least one record template, and an active technician — miss any of those and the scheduling dialog stops you. [Schedule a job](/admin/jobs/how-to-schedule-a-job) walks the dialog step by step.

While the visit is out, the board's **Work Done** and **Signed** columns fill up as technicians finish, and your Inbox lists everything waiting on you. When a job reaches **Signed** it is yours to review and close: [Close a job](/admin/jobs/how-to-close-a-job) covers what closing does, including which jobs raise a draft invoice.

<Tip>
  If the same building needs servicing on a regular cycle, do not schedule each visit by hand. Set up a [Maintenance Contract](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview) and LiftAuth creates and assigns the jobs for you.
</Tip>
