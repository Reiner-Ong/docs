## Job statuses

A job is always in exactly one of six statuses. These are the words you see on
the status badge, in the Kanban columns, and in the status filter chips.

| Status | What it means | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | The job exists, but nobody is assigned and no time is set. | An admin schedules it. |
| **Scheduled** | One technician **or** one team is assigned, and a date and time window are set. The job now appears in the technician's app. | The technician does the work and submits it. |
| **Work Done** | The technician has submitted the work on-site. The <Tooltip cta="record" href="/start/glossary">record</Tooltip> exists but is not signed off yet. | The building manager signs — or the technician skips signing. |
| **Signed** | Sign-off is complete. The job is waiting for an admin to check it. | An admin closes it. |
| **Closed** | An admin has reviewed and closed the job. Final. | Nothing. Closed jobs cannot be changed. |
| **Cancelled** | The job will not happen. Final, and the reason is stored on the job. | Nothing. Create a new job if the visit still needs to happen. |

**Closed** and **Cancelled** are the two end states. Neither can be undone, and
neither can be moved back.

<Note>
  Most jobs never sit in **Open** for long. A job created by the **Schedule Job**
  button is created and scheduled in one step, so it starts life at
  **Scheduled**. **Open** is what you see when a job was created some other way —
  for example by converting a quote — and still has nobody assigned.
</Note>

## The Jobs page

**Jobs** in the left sidebar. Two views of the same data, switched with the two
small icon buttons at the top right.

### Kanban view (the default)

One column per status: **Scheduled**, **Work Done**, **Signed**, **Closed**, and
**Cancelled**. The Cancelled column is hidden when there is nothing in it. Each
column shows a count and loads 20 jobs at a time as you scroll.

Cards are read-only. You cannot drag a card from one column to another — a job
moves status only when somebody performs the matching action.

### List view

A table with these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Job ID** | The job's short reference, for example `JOB-0142`. |
| **Lift** | The building name of the job's first lift. A job covering more than one lift reads `Marina Tower +2 more`. |
| **Technician** | The assigned technician. A dash means nobody is assigned. |
| **Scheduled Window** | Date and time window. A visit that spans several days reads `(+2 more days)`. |
| **Status** | The status badge. |
| **Plan / Created by** | The <Tooltip cta="maintenance contract" href="/start/glossary">maintenance contract</Tooltip> that generated the job, or the name of the person who created it. |

Click any row to open the job.

### Filters

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range button** (reads `All dates` when empty) | Limits the board and the table to jobs scheduled inside the range. Defaults to the current month. Pick a month, use **Next 30 days**, or click two days to make a custom range. A dot under a day means that day has scheduled jobs. |
| **Clear** | Removes the date range. |
| **Search box** | Matches on job ID, building, lift, or technician name. Applies to both views. |
| **Status chips** (List view only) | All, Scheduled, Work Done, Signed, Closed, Cancelled. |

## Actions on a job

Open a job from the Jobs page to see its actions in the top-right corner. Which
buttons appear depends on the status.

| Action | Available when the job is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Schedule** | Open | Assigns a technician or team and sets the date and time slot. Moves the job to **Scheduled**. |
| **Reschedule** | Scheduled | Changes the assignee, the date, or the time slot. The job stays **Scheduled**. |
| **Close Job** | Signed | Moves the job to **Closed**. |
| **Cancel** | Any status except Closed and Cancelled | Asks for a reason (required), then moves the job to **Cancelled**. |

There is no admin action for **Work Done** or **Signed**. Those two transitions
happen in the technician's app, or when the building manager opens their signing
link.

<Warning>
  Cancelling is permanent. A cancelled job cannot be reopened and produces no
  record. If the visit still needs to happen, schedule a new job.
</Warning>

You can also reschedule by dragging a job on the team calendar, but only when the
job is **Scheduled**, assigned to one technician, and fits in a single day.
Everything else has to be rescheduled from the job page, and the calendar tells
you so when you try.

## What is on a job page

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Job Details">
    * **Forms** — the records this visit produces.
    * **Lift** or **Lifts (N)** — every lift the job covers. All of them are in the same building.
    * **Technician** or **Team** — the assignee. A job has exactly one, never both.
    * **Building Manager** — who signs off and is billed.
    * **Scheduled Window** — date and time.
    * **Created**, **Created by**, **Last Updated**.
    * **Building Plan** — a link to the maintenance plan, on jobs a plan generated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lift records">
    Only on a **Scheduled** job that a crew has started recording. It lists each
    form with **Recorded** or **Pending**, and who recorded it, so you can see how
    far a multi-lift visit has got before it is submitted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Parts required">
    Only on jobs that came from a converted quote. Read-only: it lists the part,
    the quantity, and its status. Nothing is drawn from stock automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Linked Issues">
    The <Tooltip cta="issues" href="/start/glossary">issues</Tooltip> this job is
    meant to address, each with Open or Closed. See
    [Issues](/admin/issues).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Related">
    The record or records the job produced, and the invoice, once there is one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Timeline">
    Every status the job has entered, with who did it and when. A cancelled job
    also shows its reason here and in a **Cancellation** section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Who can do what

| Person | Sees the Jobs page | Creates jobs | Schedules / reschedules | Closes | Cancels | Can be assigned work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Yes | Yes | Any job | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Admin** | Yes | Yes | Any job | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Technician** | No — they work in the mobile app | Yes, from the app | Only a job they are already on | Not in the app | Not in the app | Yes |
| **Call centre** | No — they land on Dispatch | No | No | No | No | No |

Two rules are worth spelling out, because they surprise people:

* A **technician can reschedule only a job they are already assigned to** — either as its technician, or as a member of the assigned team. Once they are on it they may hand it to anyone else. Owners and admins can reschedule any job.
* A **call centre operator is read-only** everywhere in the portfolio. They can look a job up; they cannot change one.

A person can only be offered as a job's assignee when their account is **active**
and they hold the technician, admin, or owner role. Suspended and pending team
members never appear in the assignee list. An office admin picking up a visit is
normal and fully supported.

Building managers do not log in to LiftAuth at all. They receive a signing link.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I move a job backwards, from Signed to Work Done?">
    No. Statuses only move forwards. The only way out of a status other than
    forwards is **Cancel**, and that is permanent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sign on the building manager's behalf?">
    No. There is no sign action in the dashboard. The building manager signs on the
    technician's phone or through their own signing link. The technician can skip
    signing entirely, and the record then says so.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a job cover several lifts?">
    Because they are serviced together in one visit. All the lifts on a job are in
    the same building, and the job produces one record per form, not one per lift —
    a form covering several lifts produces a single record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my technician not in the assignee list?">
    Their account is not **active**, or they do not hold the technician, admin, or
    owner role. Check [Manage your team](/admin/managing-your-team).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I close a job?">
    Three things: the PDF of every record on the job is generated, any issues
    linked to the job are closed, and — if the job belongs to a maintenance plan
    or has a building manager — a draft invoice is generated. See
    [Invoices & Payments](/admin/invoices-and-payments).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two of my technicians recorded the same lift. What happens?">
    Nothing is lost. Both versions are kept, the lift is marked as contested in
    the app, and the crew has to pick which one counts before the job can be
    submitted. The office sees the job stay at **Scheduled** until they do.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
