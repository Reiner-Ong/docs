description: "Review a signed job and close it, so the records are finalised and any invoice draft is raised."

Closing is the last step of a job and the only one the office owns. It is what finalises the visit and raises the invoice draft, if the job is one that gets an invoice.

The PDFs are already there before you close: a record's PDF is generated in the background when it is **signed**, and **Download PDF** on a record has no status gate at all — it works before signing too, where the page renders a live preview of the document the building manager will see. Closing only runs a catch-up generation for records that reached close without a real signature, such as a skipped one.

You can only close a job that is **Signed**. If the job is still at **Work Done**, sign-off has not happened yet — see [How to send a record for sign-off from Drive](/admin/drive/sending-for-sign-off).

## Close from the Inbox

This is the quickest way, and it is where signed jobs queue up for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inbox">
    Click **Inbox** in the sidebar. Jobs waiting on you appear as
    **Close job · JOB-0142**, badged **Awaiting your close**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the work">
    The panel shows the signed records for the visit. Read the results, the notes
    and the photos, and check the signatures are there. Use **Download PDF** if you
    want the full document.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close it">
    Click **Close job** and confirm. The status moves to **Closed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Close from the job page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the job">
    Go to **Jobs** and open it — from the **Signed** column on the board, or by
    filtering the list to **Signed**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the record">
    Open the record under **Related** and check the results, notes, photos and
    signatures.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Close Job">
    The button is at the top right. Confirm in the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What closing does

Two things always happen, and a third sometimes does:

* **The records are finalised.** Any record that reached close without a real signature gets its PDF generated now; the rest already had one from signing. The record is now part of the lift's permanent history.
* **Linked issues are closed.** Any <Tooltip tip="A problem found on a lift, given a severity — Critical, Major or Minor — and tracked until it is resolved." cta="issue" href="/start/glossary">issue</Tooltip> attached to the job is closed. See [Issues](/admin/issues).
* **A draft invoice is sometimes raised.** Closing a job raises a draft invoice only when the job belongs to an active maintenance contract billed per job, or — with no contract — was created by converting a quote. Contracts billed annually are invoiced on their own schedule instead, and a contract set to bill nothing never drafts. Nothing goes to the customer until you issue it — see [Billing](/admin/billing/overview).

<Warning>
  Closing is final. A closed job cannot be reopened, rescheduled, or cancelled.
  Check the record before you close, not after.
</Warning>

## If something is wrong with the work

Do not close it. There is no way back out of **Closed**, and no way to edit a
record once it is signed. Your options are:

* **Cancel the job** and schedule a fresh visit. Cancelling asks for a reason and is also permanent. It destroys nothing: the records the technician already submitted are not deleted, archived or detached, and stay fully visible in each lift's record history.
* **Raise an issue** on the lift for whatever the technician missed, and let the next visit pick it up. See [Raising an issue](/admin/issues).
