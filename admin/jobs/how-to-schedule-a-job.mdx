Use this when a visit needs to happen and no <Tooltip tip="Your servicing agreement with a building: which lifts are covered, how often they are visited, which forms are used, and how the work is billed." cta="maintenance contract" href="/start/glossary">maintenance contract</Tooltip> has already created the job for you — a breakdown call-out, a repair, or a one-off service.

## Before you start

You need all three of these, or the dialog will not let you finish:

* A **building** with at least one lift assigned to it, and a **building manager** assigned to that building. See [Adding Lifts](/admin/lifts) and [Adding Building Managers](/admin/building-managers).
* At least one **record template** — the form the technician fills in on-site. If you have none, the dialog tells you: _"No records yet — create one under Record Templates before scheduling a job."_ See [Record Templates](/admin/record-templates/overview).
* At least one **active** technician, admin, or owner to assign the work to. See [Manage your team](/admin/managing-your-team).

<Warning>
  Assign a building manager to the building **before** you start: a building with no building manager cannot have jobs scheduled against it. Pick such a building in the dialog and step 1 is blocked — **Next** is disabled, and an amber **Building manager required** notice appears with an **Assign a building manager** link that takes you to the building. See [Adding Building Managers](/admin/building-managers).
</Warning>

## Schedule the job

Go to **Jobs** in the sidebar and click **Schedule Job**. The dialog has three
steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1 — Build your job">
    Search for the **building** by name or address and select it. Everything on
    this job happens at one building.

    Then set up the <Tooltip tip="One record template paired with the lifts it covers." cta="coverage groups" href="/start/glossary">coverage groups</Tooltip> for the visit — the dialog labels this section **Records**, and its step **Records**, but what you are building here is the instruction for the technician, not the report. Each group is a pair: one <Tooltip tip="The everyday word for a record template once it is attached to a job — the thing a technician actually fills in." cta="form" href="/start/glossary">form</Tooltip> (a record template), and the lifts it covers. The records themselves only exist once the technician submits the work.

    * Choose the record template.
    * Tick the lifts it applies to.
    * Add another group if the visit runs more than one form.

    A lift can only be in one group on the job. Some templates cover one lift
    only — if you tick two, the dialog says so and you cannot continue.

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2 — When & who?">
    Pick **one technician or one team**. A job has exactly one assignee — never
    both, and never two people.

    Set the **date**, the **start time** (Singapore time, between 06:00 and
    20:00), and the **duration**.

    When you pick a technician, their existing bookings appear on a calendar
    beside the picker so you can see clashes. A clash does not block you: the
    dialog says *"Pick another time, or schedule it anyway,"* and you can choose
    **Continue anyway**.

    A team job skips the clash check, because a team has more than one calendar.

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3 — Review & schedule">
    Check the building, the forms and their lifts, the date and duration, and
    the assignee. Use the step numbers at the top to go back and change anything.

    Click **Schedule**. The job is created directly at **Scheduled** and opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The technician sees it in their app straight away, and gets a push notification for the assignment.

Scheduling also notifies the **building manager's firm** — every user of that firm, by push and by email. It is firm-wide, not a single named contact. You are not notified yourself. Rescheduling later sends the same firm-wide push and email again, still worded as "scheduled".

## Long visits that span more than one day

If the duration you set does not fit in one working day, LiftAuth splits the
visit across consecutive working days automatically. The dialog tells you how
many days it will take, and the job page shows the schedule as
`Mon 3 Feb · 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (+2 more days)`.

A multi-day visit is still one job, with one set of records and one sign-off.

## Change the plan afterwards

* **Different person, day, or time** — open the job and click **Reschedule**. Available while the job is **Scheduled**.
* **Not happening at all** — open the job and click **Cancel**. You must give a reason, and it cannot be undone.

Details of both are in the [Jobs reference](/admin/jobs/jobs-reference#actions-on-a-job).

## If a maintenance plan already covers this building

You do not need to schedule anything. The contract creates and assigns each visit on its own schedule. See [Maintenance Contracts](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview).
