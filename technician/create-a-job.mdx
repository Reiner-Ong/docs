title: "How to create a job from your phone"
description: "Log an unscheduled visit — a breakdown call-out or anything the office has not booked."
icon: "circle-plus"
---

If you are at a lift and there is no job for the visit, create one yourself. It
takes about twenty seconds.

## Start it

There are three ways in. Use whichever is closest to hand.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="The + button on the Jobs tab" icon="plus">
    Tap **+**, then **Create job**. You pick the building, then the lift.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From a lift" icon="elevator">
    Open the lift and tap **Create job** in its actions. The lift is already
    chosen for you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From a building" icon="building">
    Open the building and tap **Create job** in its actions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Fastest way to the right lift when you are already standing at it: tap the
  **Scan** tab and scan the BCA PTO QR code on the compliance plate. If the lift
  is not registered yet, the app walks you through adding it first. If the code
  will not scan, tap **Enter URL** at the top right and paste the BCA PTO URL.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  If the building has no building manager assigned, you cannot create a job for
  it. The app says **No building manager**. Call the office and ask them to assign
  one.
</Warning>

## Fill it in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select job type">
    You pick the **form** the visit should run, grouped under MAINTENANCE,
    BREAKDOWN, REPAIR and REGISTRY JOBS. Pick the one that matches what you are
    about to do.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the lifts">
    Some forms cover several lifts at once — tick the ones this visit covers.
    Others cover exactly one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign to">
    Defaults to **Just me**. Change it if the job is for another technician or a
    team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="When">
    Choose **Start now** for a visit happening right now, or **Schedule for later**
    and pick the date and time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create it">
    The button says what will happen: **Start Job** when it is yours and starting
    now, **Book Job** when it is yours and scheduled later, or **Assign Job** when
    it is for someone else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

| What you chose | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Just me** + **Start now**, one lift | The record form opens immediately. The job is created when you submit the record — so this works with no signal at all. |
| **Just me** + **Start now**, several lifts | The job is booked for the next two hours and the hub opens. Needs a connection. |
| **Just me** + **Schedule for later** | The job is booked for the time you picked and appears in your calendar. Needs a connection. |
| **Someone else** | The job is booked and assigned to them. It appears on their phone, not yours. Needs a connection. |

From here it is the same as any other job:
[do the work and submit it](/technician/doing-a-job), then
[get it signed off](/technician/signing-off-a-job).

## Reporting a fault without a visit

If you have spotted a problem but are not doing the work, raise an issue instead
of creating a job. Tap **+** then **Create issue**, or use the actions on the
lift. See [Raise an issue](/technician/reporting-a-fault).
