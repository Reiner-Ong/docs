This is the whole on-site flow, from opening the app to submitting the work.
Getting the building manager's signature comes after, and is on its own page:
[Signing off a job](/technician/signing-off-a-job).

## Find your job

Tap **Jobs** at the bottom of the screen.

* The **Calendar** shows your day. Tap the job.
* Tap the toggle for **Pending** if you would rather see a list. It has three tabs: **Upcoming**, **Work Done** and **Signed**.

A job card shows the date and time, the building, and how many lifts the visit
covers. A red flashing tile means the visit includes breakdown work. A tick means
the job is downloaded for offline use.

Check the building and the lifts look right before you start. If they are wrong,
call the office — do not start the job.

## Start the record

Tap **Start Record**.

<Note>
  There is one button, whatever kind of work it is. Jobs no longer have a type —
  the forms do. You will see the right form once you start.
</Note>

If the job covers a single lift with a single form, you get a preparation screen
first. Any open <Tooltip cta="issues" href="/start/glossary">issues</Tooltip> on
that lift are listed, and you can link the ones you are going to deal with. Linked
issues come back at the end so you can say what happened to each.

Every other job opens the **hub** — one row per form on the visit, showing what is
recorded and what is still outstanding.

## Fill in the record

Work through the form. Depending on the form you may see pass/fail items, text,
numbers, dropdowns, dates and photo items.

* Every item takes a **note** and its own **photos**.
* Tap the camera icon on an item to take a photo or pick one from your gallery. Get close and make sure it is well lit.
* A record holds up to **96 photos**. If you go over, the app tells you which lift is over and by how much, and you have to remove some before it will submit.

Your work saves to the phone as you go. If the app closes, or the visit runs into
the next day, you get it back with a **Draft restored** banner.

At the bottom of the form you record what happened to any linked issues —
**Resolved** or **Not Resolved** — and then draw **your own** signature. Your
signature is required on every record you submit. It is not the building
manager's signature; that comes later.

## Submit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit each lift">
    Tap **Submit lift** on a row when that form is finished. You will see
    *"Lift submitted. The job stays open until every lift is in."* This works
    offline — it queues and sends itself when you get signal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the job">
    When every row is in, tap **Submit job**. The job moves to **Work Done** and
    you go to the sign-off screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Submit job** only works online. It has to bring every crew member's records
  together on the server, so it cannot be queued. Submitting individual lifts
  works offline; finishing the job does not.
</Warning>

## Working a job with a crew

On a team job, each of you records the lifts you do and submits them. The job
stays open until all of them are in — whoever is last taps **Submit job**, and it
counts for the whole crew.

**Submit job** will refuse in three cases, and tells you which:

| The app says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A lift is **contested** | Two of you recorded the same lift. Open the chooser and pick which one counts. |
| A lift is **parked** | That record was rejected by the server. Open it and fix it. |
| A lift is **not covered** | Nobody has recorded it yet. Do it, or ask whoever has it to submit. |

### Choosing between two records

When two of you recorded the same lift, the app shows **Which record counts?**
with both versions side by side — who recorded it, when they were on-site, when
they uploaded, and how many items and photos each has. Nothing is pre-selected.

Pick one and tap **Confirm choice**.

<Warning>
  The records you do not choose are deleted for good, along with their photos.
  Look at both before you choose.
</Warning>

Once every contest is settled you will see *"All sorted. Tap Submit job again to
finish."*

## Working offline

You can download jobs, lifts and forms in advance and work with no signal.
Filling in records and submitting individual lifts both work offline and sync when
you reconnect — a row shows **NOT SENT** if something failed, and you tap it to
retry.

These need a connection and will tell you so rather than queue:

* **Submit job**
* Choosing between two records for the same lift
* Creating a multi-lift job with **Start now**
* Assigning a job to someone else
