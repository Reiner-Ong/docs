---
title: "How to get the job signed off"
description: "Get the building representative's signature on your phone, send them a link, or skip signing."
icon: "signature"
---

After you submit the job it sits at **Work Done**, waiting for the building side
to sign. You have three options, and the job reaches **Signed** either way.

<Note>
  Your own signature is a different thing. You sign each record when you submit
  that lift, and that is not optional. This page is about the **building
  representative's** signature on the finished job.
</Note>

## Option 1 — They sign on your phone

Best when the building representative is standing next to you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hand them the phone">
    On the sign-off screen, they enter their name and draw their signature.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit">
    The job moves to **Signed**. This works offline — it queues and sends when you
    get signal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2 — Send them a signing link

Best when they are not on-site but will sign today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the link">
    On the sign-off screen, use the signature link. **Share** opens your phone's
    share sheet so you can send it by message or email, or you can copy it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave it with them">
    Tap **Sign later**. The job stays at **Work Done** until they open the link and
    sign — then it moves to **Signed** on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The link opens in their browser. They do not need a LiftAuth account or an app.

<Tip>
  If they say they never got it, ask the office to resend it from the record. See [Chasing a signature](/admin/drive/sending-for-sign-off).
</Tip>

## Option 3 — Skip signing

Use this when nobody on the building side is going to sign at all, and you need
to close the visit out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Skip signing">
    A confirmation appears: *"The job will be completed without a building
    representative's signature. The sign-off section will be left out of the
    record."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Skip signing** again to confirm. Offline the button reads **Queue skip
    signing** and it sends when you reconnect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The job moves to **Signed**. LiftAuth records that you skipped it and when, and
the record's PDF has no building-manager sign-off section.

<Warning>
  Skipping cannot be undone from the app, and the record cannot be signed
  afterwards. If there is any chance they will sign, send the link and tap
  **Sign later** instead.
</Warning>

If a real signature happens to land at the same moment you skip — they opened the
link while you were tapping — the real signature wins and is kept.

## Which to use

| Situation | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| They are on-site | Option 1 — sign on your phone |
| They are contactable but not here | Option 2 — send the link, tap **Sign later** |
| Nobody will sign, and the office knows | Option 3 — skip signing |

## What happens after

The job sits at **Signed** until the office reviews and closes it. Once it is
closed the record becomes part of the lift's permanent history, and you cannot
change it any more.
