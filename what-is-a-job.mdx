A job is one visit's worth of work at one building. Every time a technician goes out — for routine service, a breakdown, or a repair — that visit is a job.

## What a job is made of

A job is a container. It holds four things:

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Building** | Where the visit happens. One job covers one building. |
| **Lifts** | Which lifts are being worked on. A job can cover several lifts at that building, not just one. |
| **Forms** | What the technician is asked to fill in on site. These come from your <Tooltip tip="The form design that decides what a technician is asked on site — the items to check, the readings to take, the photos to attach." cta="record template" href="/start/glossary">record templates</Tooltip>. |
| **Assignee** | Who is doing it — one technician, or one team of them. |

The job itself does not have a type. Whether a visit counts as maintenance, a breakdown or a repair comes from the form attached to it. That is why you pick forms rather than a job type when you schedule work.

<Note>
  If you have used LiftAuth for a while, this is a change worth knowing about. Jobs used to be one lift and one fixed type. They are now a building plus whichever lifts and forms that visit needs.
</Note>

## The life of a job

Every job moves through the same statuses. You will see these words on screen.

| Status | What it means | What moves it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | The job exists, but nobody is assigned and no time is set. | An admin schedules it. |
| **Scheduled** | A technician or team is assigned and a date and time window are set. It now shows in the technician's app. | The technician does the work and submits it. |
| **Work Done** | The work is submitted. The record exists but is not signed off. | The building manager signs — or the technician skips signing. |
| **Signed** | Sign-off is complete. Waiting for an admin to check it. | An admin closes it. |
| **Closed** | An admin has reviewed and closed it. Saved to the lift's history. | Nothing. Closed jobs cannot be changed. |
| **Cancelled** | The visit will not happen. The reason is stored on the job. | Nothing. Create a new job if it still needs doing. |

**Closed** and **Cancelled** are the two end states, and neither can be undone.

<Tip>
  Most jobs never sit in **Open** for long — the **Schedule a Job** button creates and schedules in one step, so those jobs start at **Scheduled**. You see **Open** when a job arrived some other way and still has nobody on it.
</Tip>

For the full list of columns, filters, actions and who is allowed to do what, see the [jobs reference](/admin/jobs/jobs-reference).

## The three kinds of visit

The stages above are the same whichever kind of work it is. What changes is what the technician does and which record comes out of it.

### Maintenance

Routine service — the regular check-ups on every lift. Usually created for you by a [maintenance contract](/admin/maintenance-contracts/overview).

## A few important things

**The building manager signs for themselves.** You cannot sign on their behalf from the dashboard. There are three ways it happens: on the technician's phone on site, through a signing link the technician shares afterwards, or by an owner or admin emailing the request from the record in [Drive](/admin/drive/sending-for-sign-off).

**A technician can finish without a signature.** If nobody is available to sign, the technician can skip signing. The job still reaches **Signed**, but the PDF carries no building-manager signature. Skipping is final — the record can never be sent for signature afterwards. Note the dashboard shows it as **Signed**, the same as a record the building manager really signed; only the PDF shows the difference.

**Closed jobs become part of the lift's history.** Once closed, a job is saved against that lift permanently. A cancelled job is not.

**A crew can split the work.** When a job is assigned to a team, each member can submit their own part. If two people record the same lift, that lift is **contested** and the job cannot be submitted until somebody settles it.

Settling it happens in the technician app, at submit time — a crew member picks the record that counts. There is no chooser in the dashboard, so this is not something the office can sort out later. **The records not picked are deleted permanently, along with their photos.** Any faults they logged are kept.
